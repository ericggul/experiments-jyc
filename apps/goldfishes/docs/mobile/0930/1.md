# Goldfishes mobile/0930/1

Route: `/mobile/0930/1` · 2026-09-30

Short description: **Grid skeleton · tap a cross to place and configure a keyword bubble.**

```text
question: can the bare attention grid become a phone input surface for placing keywords?
baseline: /screen/2d/1 (dark field, cross grid marks, primary grid scale 2)
mutation: goldfish, media cells, selection trace and Leva removed; the cross grid is confined to a centred 16:9 map and each cross accepts a tap
invariants: 2d/1 cell rule (clamp(round(min(w,h)/30), 20, 30) × 2, centred, applied to the map) and cross mark size
evidence: tapping a cross places one bubble there and opens its settings; further crosses accept further bubbles
```

## Behaviour

- The map is a centred 16:9 box (viewport width minus 24px on a portrait phone). Only crosses at least 8px inside it are drawn and tappable.
- A tap on the map snaps to the nearest cross. An empty cross receives an unnamed bubble and selects it; an occupied cross selects its bubble.
- The selected bubble's settings appear in the empty space below the map: name, size (0.5–3 cells in diameter), weight (0–100%, shown as white fill opacity). Every change applies immediately; there is no confirm or delete control.
- A tap outside the map deselects. Bubbles are stored as map ratios and re-snapped on resize.
- State is local to the page session; nothing is persisted or transmitted yet.

## Visual register

Black and white only, following portfolio-v4 `/oi`: `#000` field, Menlo 13px/1.35, `#ddd`/`#999` text, no radii, shadows or blur, no title or confirm button. The edited bubble takes spring-in corner brackets (after the flight visualisation) and an inverted label. Crosses are white at 50% instead of 2d/1's warm off-white ink. Settings rows are 44px tall.

## Open

- The keyword role of name and weight is reserved for a later experiment; the current weight only changes fill opacity.
- Not yet observed on a physical phone, including how the iOS keyboard treats the name field below the map. A landscape phone leaves little room below the map.
