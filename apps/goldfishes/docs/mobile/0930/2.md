# Keyword bubbles — 2026-09-30

Experimental route: `/mobile/0930/2`. Independent implementation in `components/mobile/0930/2/`; `/1` remains preserved.

Question: how do configured tech-hype word pairs behave as touchable, colliding objects with finite lifetimes? The initial black surface contains only +. It opens a native modal dialog with two iOS-style scroll-snap word wheels, seven color choices and create/cancel actions. The columns combine independently (initially Artificial Intelligence). Bubbles display the pair on two lines in their chosen color.

Each bubble starts at 84px diameter and shrinks linearly to zero over 30 seconds from creation, including background time. Touch does not renew its lifetime. Creation finds a free location; at most 12 coexist. A full surface refuses creation rather than overlapping or deleting another bubble.

Dragging pushes neighbouring bubbles. Release momentum stays inside the field, reflecting from its edges and transferring through collisions with damping. Collision radii follow the shrinking visible circles. Bounded 4ms integration substeps and iterative separation handle contact chains; unresolved dense steps retain the last nonoverlapping positions. The field reserves space for +. On viewport resize it repacks without overlap and scales to retain capacity on small screens. Arrow keys push a focused bubble. Animation stops when empty or hidden; absolute creation times preserve lifetime on return.

The earlier upward-launch/removal interaction was explicitly withdrawn. There is no offscreen sending or screen transport in this version; disappearance occurs only through lifetime expiry.

Verification: four pure tests cover impact momentum, 600 frames of fast dragging through 12 bubbles, wall reflection and independent lifetime expiry. App typecheck and scoped ESLint passed. Browser/device interaction, wheel feel, visual appearance and performance remain unverified.
