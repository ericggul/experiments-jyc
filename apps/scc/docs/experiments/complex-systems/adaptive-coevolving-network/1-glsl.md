# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/1-glsl` — coevolving voters as one continuous gel (2026-10-05)

- **Question:** does route 1's network read as one connected, organic body rather than separate vertices and edges if voters and ties are drawn as a single 2D field, with each tie growing out of its voters like a neural process or tentacle?
- **Baseline:** [`/adaptive-coevolving-network/1`](README.md#route-1-the-closed-loop). `model.ts`, its tests and `layout.ts` are copies of route 1's, the model with one change: its population cap is 5,000 instead of 800 (2026-10-06, user request); defaults, rates, force law, interaction and slider are unchanged. Strictly 2D (user requirement, 2026-10-06): no depth, orbit or relief shading.
- **Changed variable:** rendering, sizes, palette and ground. Every voter (disc) and tie (curved ribbon, 40 cosine-spaced segments) adds `exp(−d/k)` of its signed distance into one float field; `−k·ln Σ` is the exponential smooth-minimum of all distances, so the composite reads one signed distance field per device pixel: crisp anti-aliased edges, smooth fillets where processes leave cells, colour as the weights' average hue. k = .55 CSS px; exponents are capped at 9.5 so sums stay finite. Shading is flat 2D with no outlines: light rises smoothly with depth (dim thin processes, bodies glowing from within), a faint haze that ends inside every splat's reach, tone-mapped on black. The anti-aliasing width is clamped to 1.5 CSS px so splat quad borders cannot show.
- **Encoding:** a tie's half-width is measured in pixels from each end: a root as wide as the voter (.92 of its radius) narrows smoothly, `exp(−(s / min(1.9r + 2, 8))^1.4)`, toward the tie's own middle width, so voter and tie read as one teardrop body; each tie also breathes (±22%) and carries a slow swelling wave (±16%) away from its roots, so thickness keeps changing, so short ties are fleshy bridges and long ties faint gossamer; long ties are also dimmed with length, as route 1 fades its lines. Ties are quadratic curves whose bend sways (two incommensurate periods). Ties across a disagreement carry a travelling brightening of their own hue (with a .5 px swell), where route 1 used ink. Voter radius grows with degree and has a stable, roughly log-normal per-voter spread (.55–2.2×; user request 2026-10-06: sizes need not be uniform). A rewired tie grows from the moving voter over .3 s; the severed tie's halves withdraw over .9 s; adoption, drift, planting and arrivals swell and brighten a voter. Route 1's six hues become restrained luminous tones: coral, azure, amber, jade, lavender, rose. Slider and hint use 1-3d's dark mono styling. Reduced motion freezes sway and beads, drops marks and slows the model to .3.
- **Options (2026-10-06):** a bottom `options`/`close` text toggle in route 2's grammar, closed by default; every bottom element lives inside it (2026-10-06, user request). Open, it shows the hint and a `thin`/`thick` tie selector, route 1's adopt–rewire slider and a voters slider (50–5,000, step 10, default 500) that starts the network over with that many voters (route 1's random construction, mean degree 4); taps can still grow the population to 5,000. `thin` (the default since 2026-10-06, user request) is the earlier build; `thick` is the later, breathing build. `thin`: taper `exp(−(s / (1.3r + 1.2))^1.6)`, still widths, middle half-width `(.3/.36 + .8t³)·clamp(30/length, .3, 1)`.
- **Budget (raised 2026-10-06 at the user's request for steady quality):** DPR up to 2, frames paced to at most 60 Hz on whole vsyncs (every other frame on a 120 Hz screen; the earlier 24 Hz cap alternated 2- and 3-vsync gaps and juddered), one RGBA32F target (RGBA16F without `EXT_float_blend`), three draw calls per frame, up to 16,384 ribbons and 8,192 discs written in place with no per-frame object allocation. Requires WebGL2 with `EXT_color_buffer_float`; otherwise, or if a shader fails to compile, the canvas stays black and the console says why. This exceeds the [GPU safety budget](../../../../../../docs/harness/webgpu-tsl-particles.md) defaults, so its 30-second visible observation is still owed.
- **Play area (2026-10-06, user request):** at large populations voters piled against the layout's top and bottom clamp lines (4% inside the frame, which also excluded an 88 px control band). The layout frame is now the whole screen and voters are held only at its edges; the options float over the field. Forces are otherwise unchanged.
- **Large populations (2026-10-06, user request: keep everything up to 2,000 exactly as is, optimise beyond):** above 2,000 voters the same picture is computed more cheaply. (1) Repulsion is summed with a Barnes–Hut quadtree (opening angle .75, leaves of 6, buffers reused across frames) instead of all pairs; springs and gravity are unchanged. (2) Splats reach 9.5k instead of 15k past their edge, trimming only the haze's last fraction of a pixel. (3) The field is RGBA16F. (4) The pixel ratio steps 2 → 1.5 → 1.25 only while the smoothed frame interval stays above 21.5 ms for 45 frames, and steps back after 360 frames under 17.5 ms. At or below 2,000 none of this runs.
- **Measured, Node only (2026-10-06, user's Mac):** exact layout 0.3 / 2.8 / 11.2 / 38.8 ms per frame at 500 / 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000 voters; Barnes–Hut 1.9 ms at 2,000 and 6.0 ms at 5,000. Per step its velocity change differs from the exact one by 3.8% (2,000) and 7.9% (5,000) on average, but after 10 s from the same start at 3,000 voters the two layouts agree to 0.4 px mean position, with equal RMS radius (199 px) and mean tie length (108 px). Mean tie length at 5,000 voters is about 160 px, so GPU fill, not tie count, is the expected limit; it is estimated, not measured.
- **History:** v3 at first (2026-10-06) had neon tube outlines on every cell, roots only half a voter wide (a visible neck at each junction) and saturated rainbow hues with white beads; the user rejected it as kitsch, discontinuous and cheap-looking. v1 (2026-10-05; thresholded density, straight capsules, white ground, ink midpoints, relief shading) and v2 (2026-10-06 morning; density membrane, glow, curl-noise ink trail, noise warp) were both rejected by the user as low-fi, not fluid or organic, with unsteady frame rate. In Chrome, v1's dense centre showed black blots and v2 first rendered black from NaNs (`pow` of a slightly negative `sin(π)` in fp32). Earlier, v3's first build failed because `template` is reserved in GLSL ES.
- **Observed (2026-10-06, user's Mac, Chrome over HTTPS, DPR 2):** the current version renders with no console or WebGL errors and no splat seams. Cells of varied size flow into tapering processes with no outline; large hubs with many ties read as star-shaped somata; long ties are faint hairlines. The automation tab was `hidden`, so its animation loop was paused: motion, interaction feedback, island formation and frame times were not observed.
- **Unresolved:** frame intervals at 2,000–5,000 voters in a visible tab (the automation tab stayed hidden; 5,000 voters rendered with no errors); a visible 30-second run with frame times at 500 and 800 voters; whether the rewired islands read as separate organisms.
