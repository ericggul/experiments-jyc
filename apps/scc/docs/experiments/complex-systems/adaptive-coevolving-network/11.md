# Route 11: awareness and infection on two layers

Route: `/adaptive-coevolving-network/11`. Date: 2026-10-02.
Component: `components/complex-systems/adaptive-coevolving-network/11/` (`AwarenessMultiplexEleven`).

## Model card

- **Object and boundary:** a deliberately synthetic abstraction. It does not make an empirical epidemiological claim. There are 240 people and two layers over the same people. On the virtual layer (who tells whom), news of a disease spreads and is forgotten. On the physical layer (who meets whom), the disease spreads and people recover.
- **Base model:** UAU–SIS from Granell, Gómez and Arenas, ["Dynamical interplay between awareness and epidemic spreading in multiplex networks"](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.128701), *PRL* 111, 128701 (2013). Infected people become aware at once, so each person is in one of three states: unaware susceptible, aware susceptible, or aware infected.
- **Adaptive extension (this route's own):** an aware susceptible person cuts a physical tie to an infected contact and links to a random susceptible stranger, the rewiring rule of Gross, D'Lima and Blasius ([PRL 96, 208701, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.208701)). Here awareness on the other layer triggers it. This closes the loop: `awareness → which contacts survive → who can be infected → awareness`. The virtual layer is fixed.
- **Rules:** continuous time, steps of at most `Δ = .05`, with event probability `1 − e^(−rate·Δ)`. Every process reads the state at the start of the step.
  - per virtual tie A–U: U becomes aware at rate `λ`;
  - per aware, non-infected person: forgets at rate `δ = .3`;
  - per physical tie S–I: infection at rate `β = .1` if S is unaware, or `γβ` with `γ = .5` if S is aware. If no infection happens and S is aware, avoidance happens at rate `w`: S rewires the tie to a random susceptible non-contact;
  - per infected person: recovers at rate `μ = .25` and stays aware;
  - import from outside at `.0004` per healthy person.
- **Graphs:** the physical layer is a random graph with mean degree 6, every person tied at least once. The virtual layer has mean degree 4. Half of it copies initial physical ties, which echoes Granell et al., where the virtual layer contains the physical one; the rest are random long-range ties. At the start, 3% of people are infected (and so aware). Seed `0x5bd1e995`.
- **Parameters:** `λ ∈ [0, .6]`, default `.2`. `w ∈ [0, 1]`, default `.5`.
- **Macro observables:** infected share, aware share, the share of contacts joining a healthy and an infected person, and the overlap of physical and virtual ties.

## Screen

- **Field:** white, ink `#11110f`, two accents: red `214,58,34` for infection and blue `38,92,196` for awareness and news. There is no title, legend or counter.
- **Two copies of each person:** every person is drawn twice. The physical copy is a filled dot, red if infected. Its area grows with the number of contacts. The virtual copy is a faint ink presence dot with a blue ring if the person is aware. In 겹침 the two copies coincide, so an aware infected person is a red dot inside a blue ring.
- **Ties:** news ties are thin, dashed and blue, at alpha .16. Contacts use route 2's encoding: healthy–healthy in faint ink, infected–infected in faint red, healthy–infected in strong red.
- **Event marks (.9 s):**
  - *News:* a blue line is drawn out from the teller to the listener along the virtual tie.
  - *Infection:* a red ring expands.
  - *Avoidance:* a dashed red trace to the abandoned infected contact while the new ink tie is drawn out to the stranger.
  - *Announcement:* a blue ring expands.
  - Forgetting and recovery have no mark; the ring or the red simply disappears.
- **Views (inside 옵션):**
  - *겹침* (default): the force layout of physical contacts only.
  - *층:* the same layout scaled into two panels, side by side when wide (소통 left) and stacked when tall (소통 on top). A person sits at matching relative spots in both, as one constant translation, so news running ahead of infection can be compared directly.
  - A switch eases every copy over .9 s (cubic in-out) from a snapshot; after that, points follow at rate 12/s. Dots shrink toward √(panel scale) as the layers split. Panel labels 소통 and 접촉 cross-fade.
- **Tempo:** 1 model unit per second, so an infection lasts about 4 s. This is slower than route 2 because news events outnumber infections several times; at default, about 40 news marks per second fire while awareness is endemic.
- **Reduced motion:** tempo .3 and no event marks.
- **Layout:** layout is presentation only and never feeds back into the model.

## Interaction

- A tap on a person (either copy) infects them.
- Pressing and holding for 380 ms on a person, or dragging more than 8 px, *announces*: each person touched becomes aware, together with all of their news contacts.
- On the focused canvas, Enter or Space infects the person nearest the centre, and A announces from that person.
- A one-line hint, 누르면 감염, 길게 누르거나 쓸면 소식 알림, stays until the first interaction.

## Measured, pure model only (2026-10-02)

240 people, seeds 7919, 15838, 23757, 31676 and 39595, initial 3%, no importation.

- **Mean infected share at 80 units:**

  | `w` \ `λ` | 0 | .05 | .1 | .2 | .3 | .6 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0 | .40 | .34 | .35 | .31 | .20 | .14 |
  | .5 | .30 | .29 | .16 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
  | 1 | .29 | .22 | .04 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

  Below the information threshold (about `δ/⟨k_v⟩ ≈ .06–.08`), awareness exists only around infected people. Above it, awareness is endemic by itself (about 55–80% aware) and, together with avoidance, ends the outbreak. Without avoidance, news alone reduces prevalence only partly (protection `γ` alone).
- **Default (`λ = .2`, `w = .5`):** a 3% outbreak peaks at 4–17% and dies out after 16–48 units (seconds on screen).
- **News outruns the disease:** with no protective response, from a single case at `λ = .2`, 20% of people are aware after 3.5–6 units, and 20% are infected after 6.5–25 units.
- **Bistability:** an endemic state established at `λ = .03` (about 25–33% infected) is not cleared by `λ = .15`, and only sometimes by `.2`. At `λ = .3` it clears in 23–67 units, and at `.6` in 16–48 units.
- **Layer decoupling:** in the endemic state (`λ = .03`, seed 7919), 1372 avoidances in 60 units cut physical–virtual overlap from .34 to .06. At default, about 30 avoidances leave it at .32.
- **Cost:** model, layout and views per frame in Node at 1440×760 average .14–.23 ms across regimes, with rare single-frame spikes up to 16 ms (GC/JIT, not reproducible per regime). This has not been profiled in a browser.

## Tests

`model.test.ts`:

- deterministic replay;
- news is passed only by an aware virtual contact; avoidance only by an aware healthy person, from an infected to a healthy person; contact count conserved, simple graph, virtual layer unchanged, infected ⇒ aware;
- news reaches 20% before infection does on every seed;
- `λ = .03` is endemic while `λ = .3` and the default reach extinction;
- at `λ = .2`, `w = 0` is endemic while `w = .5` reaches extinction;
- with `γ = 1, w = 0`, news does not change prevalence;
- raising `λ` to .6 clears an established epidemic;
- announcement informs exactly the person and their news contacts.

`views.test.ts`: both copies stay in the frame in every view at desktop and phone size; 층 separates the layers along the split axis while keeping one constant offset per person.

## Unresolved

- Whether the participant can tell aware rings from infection at phone width when awareness is endemic (55–80% ringed).
- At default, news marks are dense; a lower `δ` would thin them but weakens the λ contrast (at `δ = .15`, `λ = .05` is already only 13% infected).
- The metacritical point in Granell et al. is defined with `γ = 0` and an analytic MMCA. This route uses `γ = .5` plus avoidance, so its thresholds are measured, not derived.
- Whether the adaptive awareness-triggered rewiring has a direct published counterpart; it is recorded here as this route's extension.
