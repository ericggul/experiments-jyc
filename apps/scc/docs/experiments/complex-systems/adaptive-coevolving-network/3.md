# Adaptive coevolving network / 3: adaptive cooperation

Route: `/adaptive-coevolving-network/3`. Date: 2026-10-02.
Component: `components/complex-systems/adaptive-coevolving-network/3/`.

## Model card

- **Object and boundary.** This is a synthetic abstraction, not a calibrated social model. 240 players with a fixed population and a fixed number of ties (random graph, mean degree 8, every player tied at least once). Half the players start cooperating. The model owns strategies and ties only. Positions are a browser force layout of the current ties and never feed back into the model.
- **Game.** Each player plays a weak prisoner's dilemma once with every neighbour. C–C earns 1 each; D against C earns `b` for D and 0 for C; D–D earns 0. A cooperator's payoff is its number of cooperating neighbours, and a defector's is `b` times that number. The model keeps a cooperating-neighbour count per player, so a payoff costs O(1).
- **One update** (random sequential). Pick a random player `i` and one of its ties, to `j`:
  1. **Mutation:** with probability `μ = .002`, `i` flips its strategy instead.
  2. **Partner switching:** if `j` defects, then with probability `p` player `i` drops `j` and ties itself to a random neighbour of `j` it does not know yet. If there is no such neighbour, it ties to a random stranger. This holds for cooperators and defectors alike.
  3. **Imitation:** otherwise, if `Πj > Πi` and their strategies differ, `i` copies `j` with probability `(Πj − Πi) / (b · max(ki, kj))`.
- **Why it is adaptive.** Nobody leaves a cooperator, so ties flow toward cooperators. A cooperator with many cooperating ties out-earns a defector with few, so it is copied. Defectors are abandoned and their payoff falls toward zero. The loop is `strategy → which ties survive → payoff → who is copied → strategy`.
- **Parameters.** Temptation `b ∈ [1.1, 2.5]`, default `1.6`. Switching `p ∈ [0, 1]`, default `.3`. `μ = .002`. Tempo: 2 updates per player per second (`SWEEPS_PER_SECOND`), or 0.6 under reduced motion.
- **Departures from the sources.**
  - ZES use synchronous imitate-the-best updates, and only a defector who copied a defector rewires, to a random player. A literal asynchronous port of that rule froze within about 20 sweeps. Its contrast was weak: at `b = 1.5`, `p = 0` gave about 50% cooperation against 55–90% for `p > 0`, with hub ratios of only about 2–3.
  - From Santos, Pacheco and Lenaerts, the route takes the rule that anyone leaves a defector, for a neighbour of that defector. It does not take their payoff-competitive decision about who keeps the tie: here the switch happens with a flat probability `p`.
  - The imitation probability is the Santos–Pacheco rule for accumulated payoffs.
  - Mutation is added so the steady state never freezes.

## Screen encoding

- White field. Cooperators are ink (`#11110f`) and defectors red (`rgb(205, 62, 30)`). Dot area grows with payoff (`r ∝ 0.6 + 0.2·√Π`), so the players most likely to be copied are the largest.
- C–C ties are faint ink. C–D ties are strong red because they are where exploitation happens, and they are what people walk away from. D–D ties are faint red.
- **Event marks** (fade in .9 s; none under reduced motion):
  - *Switch:* a dashed red trace runs back to the abandoned defector while the new ink tie draws out.
  - *Imitation:* a line grows from the copied neighbour to the copier, in the colour of the adopted strategy.
  - *Mutation or tap:* a ring in the new colour.
- No title, legend or counters. The share of cooperators, the mean ties of each group and the largest degree are exposed only to screen readers, updated every 2 s.
- **Views** (behind 옵션), with the /2 grammar: a .9 s cubic in-out ease from a snapshot to the live targets, then exponential following at 12/s. Labels cross-fade.
  - *네트워크:* the force layout (copied from /2), the default.
  - *서열:* radial position. Distance from the centre falls with `√(Π/64)`, and the angle is fixed by id. Imitation lines therefore run from the centre outward, and a fallen leader visibly slides out as its partners leave.
  - *연결과 보수:* ties across and payoff up, both on `√(·/64)` scales over a square plot. A cooperator with only cooperating neighbours sits on the diagonal; a defector sits above it by the factor `b`, but only while it still has cooperating ties. This shows why a well-tied cooperator outranks a defector who has been left.
  - Tie visibility is 1, .4 and .08 in the three views, with a .3 floor for C–D ties.

## Interactions

- **Tap a player** (hit radius `max(14 px, r + 6)`) to flip their strategy. A cooperator starts defecting and a defector starts cooperating. Tapping the biggest dark player is the cascade experiment.
- **Enter / Space** on the focused canvas makes the richest cooperator defect. If nobody cooperates, the best-connected player starts cooperating instead.
- **Bottom 옵션/닫기 toggle:** a Korean model description, the three view buttons (`aria-pressed`), a 작은 유혹–큰 유혹 slider (`b`) and a 머묾–떠남 slider (`p`). The hint `사람을 누르면 협력과 배신이 뒤바뀜` shows until the first flip. Canvas `touch-action: none`; a 56 px bottom band is kept clear of the layout.

## Measured pure-model results

All runs use 240 players and mean degree 8, start half cooperating, and use the default seed `0x3c6ef372` unless noted. "s" means screen seconds at 2 sweeps/s.

- **Default seed over time:**
  - `p = 0`: cooperation is .30 at 5 s, .09 at 20 s and .02 from 35 s.
  - `p = .3`: cooperation first falls to .38 at 5 s, then rises to .83 at 20 s and .93–.99 from 25 s.
  - `p = .6`: .83 at 10 s, then about .93–.99.
  - `p = 1`: .96 by 10 s.
- **After 120 sweeps, seeds `0x3c6ef372` / 2 / 3:** `p = 0` gives .025 / .063 / .067; `p = .6` gives .988 / .971 / .983.
- **Temptation:** at `b = 2.5`, `p = .1` gives .04 and `p = .6` gives .95. At `b = 2`, `p = .1` mostly fails, ending at .01–.34 after 200 sweeps. At `b = 1.2`, even `p = .1` reaches about .9. The higher the temptation, the more switching is needed.
- **Hubs:**

  | Condition | Largest degree / mean degree | Cooperators' mean degree | Defectors' mean degree |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Static network (`p = 0`) | 2.0 | – | – |
  | `p = .3`, 200 sweeps | 6.4 | 8.2 | 3.1 |
  | `p = .1` at `b ≥ 2` | up to 10–15 | – | – |

  The richest cooperator is the best-connected player.
- **Cascades** (`p = .3`, 200 sweeps, then a flip, seeds `0x3c6ef372`, 5, 6, 7):
  - Making the leader defect (degree 47–61) drops cooperation from .91–.97 to a minimum of .76–.82 within 4–18 s. Cooperation then recovers as partners leave the fallen leader.
  - Flipping an ordinary cooperator drops it by about .05.
  - At `p = 1` the leader is abandoned before the defection spreads, and the drop is ≤ .02.
- **Event rate:** about 10–30 imitation or switch events per sweep, so about 20–60 per second on screen. Rates are highest during the takeover and fall to a few per second at steady state (mostly mutation and its repair).

## Tests

`model.test.ts`:
- deterministic replay;
- switching only leaves defectors, ties are conserved and simple, and cooperative counts match a recount;
- imitation only copies a richer neighbour;
- the causal contrast: `p = 0` gives < .15 and `p = .6` gives > .85 on three seeds;
- `b = 2.5` needs more than `p = .1`;
- switching creates hubs (ratio > 4 against < 2.5 when static), and the leader is the best-connected player;
- a fallen leader causes a mean drop > .1, at least 1.5 times that of an ordinary cooperator;
- flip bounds.

`views.test.ts`:
- every view stays in frame at 1440×704 and 390×644;
- the 서열 view orders players by payoff;
- in the 연결과 보수 view, a defector sits above a cooperator with the same number of cooperating ties.

## 2026-10-03 legibility change

After a browser viewing, the user could not tell cooperators from defectors. Three causes:
- Dot area followed payoff from 0.6× base, so a defector whose cooperators had left (payoff 0) shrank to about 1.5 px.
- Defectors were drawn among cooperators and could sit under hubs.
- Strong red cooperator–defector ties made red read as everywhere.

Changes:
- Dot size now has a floor at 1× base (minimum 2.6 px), with payoff adding √Π × .16.
- Defectors are drawn last, as red discs with a white rim and an outer red ring.
- Cooperator–defector ties drop from .55 to .32 opacity.
- The first-screen hint and the model description now say 검은 점은 협력, 빨간 점은 배신.

The model is unchanged.
