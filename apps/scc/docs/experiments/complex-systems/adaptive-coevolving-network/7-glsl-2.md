# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-2` — ranked web as combed fibres (2026-10-06)

- **Question:** can route 7's PageRank loop read as one living body if links are not strokes but bundles of hairline fibres combed together, as in BarabásiLab's [150 years of Nature](https://barabasi.com/art/work/150-years-of-nature), with the rank each link passes on as the number of its fibres?
- **Baseline:** [`/adaptive-coevolving-network/7`](7.md), through [`7-glsl`](7-glsl.md) (which the user found unsatisfying: similar-sized cells, uniform hairlines, no readable hierarchy or flow). `model.ts`, `layout.ts`, `views.ts` and both of their tests are copies of 7-glsl's (page cap 1,000); every parameter, slider, view, gesture and the presentation-only surfers are route 7's.
- **Changed variable:** drawing only. Invariants kept: page area = rank × budget, link encoding = d·PR·w/W, continuity of every change, route 7's gestures and options.
  - **Fibres:** a link is `1 + 2000 · d·PR·w/W` fibres (the last carries the fraction), each a 0.32 CSS px hairline adding .11 of light to one half-float density target; `1 − e^(−.85 Σ)` maps the sum to light on near-black. A lone link is a faint grey hair; a link carrying a hub's rank, or many links combed together, glow by density alone. Total fibres ≈ d · 2000 + links.
  - **Combing:** force-directed edge bundling ([Holten & van Wijk 2009](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2009.01450.x)) run incrementally in `bundling.ts`. Each link keeps 14 inner points as displacements from its chord (so they follow the layout); per frame one overdamped step: smoothing spring 18/s, restoring pull .35/s, attraction to the matching points of its ≤ 10 most compatible links (angle × scale × position compatibility ≥ .55), peak 150 px/s at 36 px, bend capped at .32 of the chord. Partners come from a grid of midpoints, 1/15 of links per frame, ≤ 160 candidates each. A new link starts straight and combs in; a dropped one is forgotten and its partners relax back.
  - **Bundle shape:** fibres follow a Catmull–Rom curve through the points, spread across `0.5 + 1.5·√fibres` px with a slight slow wave, narrowing toward the pages and fanning over each page's dot (`.75 · r · (1−u)³` at the source, `.75 · r · u³` at the target). A growing link's fibres fade toward its tip (reach `min(1, 6w)^.6`).
  - **Pages:** flat opaque dots over the fibres, area = rank × 0.8% of the field (min radius 1.4 px), spaced by `1.2r + 4`. Focus dims other dots and links (brightness, not transparency).
  - **Surfers:** 300 sparks with five-step tails riding a random lane of their link's bundle; a teleport fades out and in at the pages.
  - **Ground:** near-black; options use 7-glsl's panel with neutral ink.
- **History (2026-10-06):**
  1. *Slime mould (rejected).* Tubes as one additive density field on agar with /amoeba/2's gel composite (refraction, absorption, wet highlight), mean-direction Bézier bundling, granular streaming along rank flow, a 14 s slime memory. Rank hierarchy did read (big amber hubs, thick trunks), but the user rejected it outright: glossy egg-yolk beads and a red focus read as kitsch, the thin tubes as a spider web, the slime traces as grey stains — the same nodes-and-lines grammar in another material.
  2. *Combed fibres (current),* chosen by the user from three proposed directions (surfer swarm, combed fibres, ink specimen). First build: high-flow bundles saturated into solid white horns pinching at dot centres → lower fibre light, wider spread, roots fanned over the dots. Translucent dots showed fibre wedges → opaque. Partner recomputation ran every frame whenever a link appeared (≈50 ms frames at 300 pages) and list indices went stale → object references, round-robin refresh.
- **Observed (2026-10-06, user's Mac, Brave over HTTPS, 1470×919, DPR 2):** no console or WebGL errors. The automation tab was `hidden`, so frames were driven by a MessageChannel shim replacing `requestAnimationFrame` (verification only; not in the code). At 100 pages, bright combed bundles run between the leading pages and faint single hairs reach the periphery; the leader changed within seconds; focus works. Main-thread frame time: 300 pages median 4.0 ms, p95 11.4 ms; 1,000 pages median 13.7 ms, p95 16.8 ms, worst 39 ms. GPU time and a visible-tab 30 s run were not measured.
- **Measured in Node (user's Mac, 600 frames at 60 Hz):** model / layout / bundling per frame 0.05 / 0.07 / 0.38 ms at 100 pages (418 links), 0.13 / 0.50 / 1.18 ms at 300 (1,315), 0.48 / 5.6 / 4.2 ms at 1,000 (4,317); worst frame 20.9 ms at 1,000.
- **Tests:** `bundling.test.ts` (parallel links comb, a crossing link stays straight, curve ends and interpolation, a dropped link is forgotten and its partner unbends) plus the copied model and view tests.
- **Unresolved:** whether the leading pages' dots (up to ~40 px) are too heavy against the fibres; whether fibres should carry a hue (BarabásiLab colours by discipline; this model has no categories); visible-tab frame pacing and GPU cost at 1,000 pages.
