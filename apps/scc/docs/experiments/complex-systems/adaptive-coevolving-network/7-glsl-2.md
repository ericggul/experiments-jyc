# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-2` — ranked web as gel in relief (2026-10-06/07)

- **Question:** can [`7-glsl`](7-glsl.md) read more as a living body, and its PageRank more directly, without leaving its grammar?
- **Baseline:** 7-glsl, copied whole (model, layout, views, tests, `tentacle.ts`, `fluid.ts`, styles). Invariants kept from it: one smooth-union distance field for pages, links and surfers, so nothing is ever drawn in front of anything else and crossings fuse; pages spaced by `1.12r + 2` so cells never overlap; area = rank at 3% of the field; grown Hermite paths; monochrome on black; route 7's gestures and options.
- **Weaknesses of 7-glsl observed in the browser (2026-10-07):** large pages read as flat grey discs pasted on the web (the field's depth cap of about 22 px saturated their light, and a root flare capped at 10 px vanished against them); every link was a similar hairline, so rank flow was not legible.
- **Changed variables:**
  - **Relief:** the field's RGB now carries brightness (R) and each primitive's own size (G: a cell's radius, a ribbon's half-width). The composite builds a height field of domes, `h = s·√(1 − (1 − depth/s)²)` with `s = min(size, .85 · depth cap)`, takes normals from neighbouring depths, and lights it (ambient .42 + .7 Lambert from the upper left, a soft rim, light rising with thickness as `1 − e^(−h/6)`) with a faint drifting cytoplasm grain deeper than 2 px. The exponent ceiling on float32 targets is 80 (about 44 px of depth) instead of 40.
  - **Roots:** a link leaves its page `min(.92r, .3r + 5)` wide (target `min(.85r, .3r + 5)`), tapering over `min(1.6 · root + 3, 60)` px with exponent 1.3, so large pages grow stout processes and small ones keep 7-glsl's teardrops.
  - **Flow:** middle half-width `.2 + 55 · d·PR·w/W` (7-glsl: `.15 + 30 ·`), and tone `.45 + .55 · min(1, √(60 · flow))`, so links that pass on more rank are wider and brighter.
  - **Lobes** act only beyond .35–.85 of a cell's radius: applied everywhere, the angle's 1/r gradient creased the relief into radial seams.
- **Observed (2026-10-07, user's Mac, Brave over HTTPS, DPR 2, hidden tab driven by a MessageChannel frame shim for verification only):** no console or WebGL errors; cells and hubs render as lit domes fused with their processes, without seams or flat plateaus; hubs exchange rank through visibly thicker arteries. Frame times were not re-measured after this change.
- **Rejected attempts on this route, same day (useful failures):**
  1. *Slime mould on agar* — additive density tubes with /amoeba/2's gel. Rejected: glossy egg-yolk beads, red focus, grey slime stains; the same nodes-and-lines grammar in another material.
  2. *Combed fibres* — hairline fibres per unit of rank flow with incremental force-directed edge bundling (Holten & van Wijk 2009, later moved into a GPU ping-pong pass), flat page dots drawn over them. Read like BarabásiLab's *150 years of Nature*, but rejected: no organic connection between node and edge, flat dots, and elements stacked over others.
  3. *Fibres with root funnels* — ribbon funnels folded into shards; bead chains read as beads. Rejected outright.
  The lesson: improve 7-glsl's one-field body rather than replace its grammar; never layer one element over another; never flat fills.
- **Unresolved:** whether the faint hairlines of weak links still read as a hairball; frame time at 300 and 1,000 pages with the relief composite (four extra field taps per pixel).
