# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-3` — ranked web as a self-patterning tissue (2026-10-07)

- **Question:** can the PageRank organism be grown rather than drawn — a tissue that patterns itself, where a page is a colony of cells and a link a bundle of fibres?
- **Baseline:** [`7-glsl`](7-glsl.md) for the model, layout, views, gestures, options and grown link paths (`tentacle.ts`), copied whole. The renderer is new (`morphogen.ts`).
- **Changed variable:** the picture is a Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion system ([Pearson, Science 261, 189 (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.261.5118.189)) run on the GPU every frame; the network only sets where and how it may grow.
  - **Territory** (half CSS resolution, two targets): pages (area = rank × 6%, route 7's budget, so a page can hold a colony) and links (grown paths, half-width `1.2 + 70 · d·PR·w/W` px, roots `middle + min(.35r, 4)`) add `exp(−d/2 px)` of their signed distance — one smooth-union body, nothing drawn over anything. It also carries link orientation as `(cos 2θ, sin 2θ)`, pageness from the pages' own distance alone, each link's drift (14 px/s, source → target, inside the link only) and surfer pulses.
  - **Parameters** once per frame: kinetics `(F, k)` — dividing spots `(.0367, .0649)` in pages, fibres `(.042, .0605)` with diffusion 1.8× faster along the link than across it in links, barren `(.03, .1)` outside — the drift per step and the seeding threshold.
  - **Morphogenesis** (float32 ping-pong, 10 steps per frame): `Du = .21`, `Dv = .105` on a 5-point stencil read from upstream (semi-Lagrangian drift). Seeds are round 5 × 5 blocks (`u = .5, v = .25`), denser in pages than links.
  - **Relief** (device resolution): `v` as a height field lit from its own normals — ivory ridges, dark valleys, the territory only a faint membrane line.
  - **Encoding:** a page's colony grows with its rank; a weak link is a membrane line too narrow for tissue, a strong one a filament or a bundle; the tissue drifts toward the page that receives the rank; surfers and newborn or focused pages pulse the tissue.
- **Measured in Node:** under these rates and stencil, a 3 × 3 seed died in every regime tried; from 5 × 5 the spot regime grew to 22% cover in 6,000 steps and the fibre regime to 59%.
- **History (2026-10-07):**
  1. A *sap* version (luminous sap advected through 7-glsl-2's gel) was rejected by the user as a lesser copy of 7-glsl-2.
  2. First morphogen build at full CSS resolution, 16 steps per frame, with all parameters recomputed per step: the user reported a very low frame rate, and the browser tab stopped responding during measurement. Moving to half resolution, a per-frame parameter pass and 10 steps fixed it.
  3. Two GLSL compile failures: local variables named `step` and `round` shadow built-ins.
  4. Pages first drowned in their links' broad roots (they read as link), and 3 × 3 seeds died; pageness from the page distance alone and 5 × 5 seeds fixed them.
- **Observed (2026-10-07, user's Mac, Brave over HTTPS, DPR 2; hidden tab driven by a MessageChannel frame shim for verification only):** no console or WebGL errors; after about 40 s the leading pages are colonies of dozens of cells and strong links carry fibre bundles. Frame cost with the GPU synchronised by a 1-pixel `readPixels`: median 5.1 ms, p95 7.8 ms, worst 26.9 ms at 100 pages.
- **Rejected (2026-10-07): domes over the pages.** To give large pages presence, each page was drawn as a translucent dome with a bright rim, its tissue made denser with size. The user rejected it as artificial and as bodies laid over everything else; it was reverted.
- **Large pages given presence within the tissue (2026-10-07, after the rejected domes):** nothing is added on top. Each page's territory channel now encodes depth as a share of its radius (`exp(4 · depth / r)`), and the conditions vary with that depth: near the rim the incoming links' fibre kinetics and orientation continue, so fibres enter the page unchanged; deeper in, where many links meet and their orientations cancel, anisotropy fades and the kinetics move to close-packed tissue `(.03, .0565)`; the core is seeded densely so it fills within seconds. A larger page is therefore a larger mass of dense tissue that its links' fibres run into and loosen into. Observed after 30 s: the two leading pages are dense tissue masses joined by a fibre bundle that fans out into both; no layer drawn over another. Measured on a fresh load: 60 fps, frame cost median 6.1 ms, p95 10.8 ms. Later measurements in the long-hidden tab stalled because the verification shim (not the route) stopped delivering frames during Fast Refresh remounts caused by another session's edits.
- **Feed follows rank (2026-10-07, user: the picture did not show PageRank's rich-get-richer at all; do not compensate by adding pages):** Gray–Scott's F is the feed rate, so it now carries the inequality. A page's vitality is its rank against the average page (PR · N, log scale, starving at .4×, fully fed at 5×); a link's is its flow against the mean link flow (d / links, .5× to 8×). A third territory target carries vitality (pages weighted by their depth, so inside a page its own vitality wins). Feed `F = .013 + .022 · vitality` at `k = .0565` — measured in Node: no tissue survives at F = .014, 17% cover at .018, 48% at .026, 80% at .034, solid at .038. Seeding follows vitality; drift along a link runs at `14 · (.3 + 1.7 · vitality)` px/s. The earlier fixed kinetics (fibres in links, packed tissue in page depth) are replaced; orientation still lines tissue up along links and fades with depth into a page. Observed: after 30 s the two richest pages were lush tissue joined by a thick feeding channel, a mid page sparse, poor pages bare; 20 s later a new leader had taken over, with feeding channels radiating from it and the former hubs reduced to rings.
- **Calmer and fuller (2026-10-07, user: flashes move too fast, other pages too faint, the picture too sparse):** the tissue was fixed to the screen while the layout moved the bodies under it, so it kept dying on one side of a body and regrowing on the other. Each page's drawn velocity (smoothed over about .1 s) now enters the transport field — pages carry their tissue, links carry it with the interpolated velocity of their ends plus the drift — so tissue rides with its body. Drawn positions follow the layout at 3/s and radii at 2/s (7-glsl: 12 and 8). Surfer pulses are a quarter as strong (.025); ground that cannot sustain tissue is never seeded; drift is `5 · (.4 + 1.6 · vitality)` px/s. Pages never starve: feed `F = .026` (48% cover) for the poorest to `.036` (about 90%) for the richest; weak links can still go bare. Observed: the leading pages are nearly filled tissue bodies joined by fibre bridges, small pages hold tissue. Screenshots of the hidden automation tab do not show motion reliably, so the pace was not judged from them.
- **Flicker, living nuclei, small pages (2026-10-07):** a frame-difference measurement (luminance change per .1 s over a 600 × 600 px region; pixels lit for under .2 s) found 12.7/255 and 158 blinks. Causes and fixes: poor pages sat in a chaotic regime (F = .026 changes by 9/255 per .1 s in Node; the page floor is now .027, about 4/255, writhing but whole, and the top is capped at .034 because .036 collapses); seeds landed in the gaps of existing tissue (a seed now needs no tissue within 8 px); surfers drew bright dots over bare ground (their pulse now only swells existing tissue, and they are drawn at 1/2.5 of 7-glsl's pace). A stable-only kinetics path, rare seeds, no surfer pulses and a slower layout were tried and rejected by the user as killing the tissue's life; they were reverted. Every page now keeps a living nucleus (`max(1.8 px, .12r)`) held in the tissue, so no page is too small to be seen; small pages may be drawn smaller (minimum radius 2.5 px) and cluster: in this route's copy of the layout, two bodies repel by the product of their presences (`clamp(√(PR·N / 4), .2, 1)`), and collision gaps and link rest lengths shrink with presence. Blinks measured 0 after the nucleus change. Small pages as protists and a wider layout were split off to [`7-glsl-4`](7-glsl-4.md) at the user's request; this route was reverted to the state before them.
- **Unresolved:** small square remnants of seeds are still briefly visible; the faint membrane lines of weak links form a grey web; frame cost at 300 and 1,000 pages; whether the drift reads as direction at a glance.
