# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-5` — PageRank performed by a surfer colony (2026-10-07)

- **Question:** can PageRank be shown by performing it — a colony of random surfers doing exactly what the definition says — so that the network is only what they leave behind?
- **Reflection on 7-glsl, -2, -3 and a first 7-glsl-5:** the routes the user accepted compute form in GLSL, never layer one element over another, keep node and link one body, keep every page visible, make the inequality of rank legible, and stay alive without flicker (judged by measured frame difference and blinks, not by eye in a hidden tab). All three still gave nodes and links a shape. A first 7-glsl-5 (a watershed: lakes, rivers, rain over a textured fractal terrain) was rejected outright as unrequested background texture, not minimal, not showing the principle, and kitsch; it was replaced. The user's requirement for this route: abstract and minimal, yet organic — self-sustaining, proliferating, generated rather than drawn — with node and link never apart.
- **Interpretation (`swarm.ts`, all GLSL):** up to 36,864 surfers (192²) live in float textures. A surfer stays .7 s inside its page's disc (area = rank × 3%), then with probability d follows one of its page's candidate links chosen by weight (cumulative table per page, 8 slots), or otherwise — or from a page without links — jumps to any page, leaving no trail across. Travelling at 95 px/s, it steers toward its target and equally toward trail already laid (Physarum-style sensing, 12 px ahead at ±.7 rad; Jones 2010), so paths bend, merge and thicken where they are used: the rich get richer in the paths themselves. Every living surfer deposits a soft 1.5 px point into a CSS-resolution trail that blurs lightly (15%) and decays over 1.2 s. The composite is the trail alone as warm light on black, `1 − e^(−1.1 T)`, with slight relief from its own slope — no background, no drawn node or link. A page glows as its visitors linger (its light follows its rank), a link is a vein as bright as its traffic (the rank it carries), and both are the same trail.
- **Proliferation:** the colony starts with 192 surfers and doubles every 1.6 s until full (about 13 s); a newborn is a copy of the surfer it divided from, so the web grows out of a few seeds. Changing the page count kills the colony and clears the trail, and it grows again.
- **Baseline kept:** route 7's model, parameters, views and gestures (links are hit-tested as straight segments, as nothing draws a path); 7-glsl-3's presence-weighted layout (floor .35 here).
- **Tuning observed in the browser (hidden tab, frame shim; 2026-10-07):** 2 px deposits at half resolution with gain .9 burned the centre to a white blur; finer full-resolution trail with a quarter of the deposit gave fine veins but nearly straight ones; trail-following stronger than aim made surfers circle in loops and miss their pages; equal weight (.6 aim, .6 trail) gives curved, merging veins that still arrive, every page a small ring of lingering surfers, and bright veins between the richest pages.
- **Measured (aim .45 / trail .8 setting, frame shim, GPU synchronised):** frame median 3.8 ms, p95 7.1 ms; frame difference 1.5/255 per .1 s; 0 blinks. Not re-measured after the final balance.
- **Unresolved:** whether the faint periphery should be brighter; the focus gesture no longer has a visible effect; frame cost with the GPU budget's 30-second visible run.
