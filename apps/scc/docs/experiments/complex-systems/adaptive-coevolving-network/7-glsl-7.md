# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-9` — ranked web as ink marbling (2026-10-07)

- **Question:** can PageRank read as suminagashi — ink dripped and drawn across still water — with pages as wells of ink and links as the currents that carry it, one ink on one sheet?
- **Baseline:** [`7-glsl-5`](7-glsl-5.md), copied whole for route 7's model, parameters, views, gestures and options and 7-glsl-3's presence-weighted layout; links are hit-tested as straight segments. The renderer is new (`marbling.ts`). Constraints carried from 7-glsl–7-glsl-5: form computed in GLSL and never flat; abstract and minimal, with no background texture; node and link one substance, nothing drawn over anything; every page visible; the inequality of rank legible; alive without flicker; 60 fps.
- **Changed variable — the water** (2D incompressible fluid, stable fluids after Stam 1999, at half CSS resolution, half-float targets):
  - **Pages drip.** Each page has a core `max(1.6, .38 r)` px across (r: rank-area radius at 3% of the field) that alternately drops ink and clear water: ink for the cycle's first half, clear for the second, at `.22 + .88 · vitality` Hz, with a per-page phase. While it drips it injects water (`5 + 11 · vitality` per second as a divergence source), so each drop pushes the earlier ones outward as concentric rings. A richer page drips faster and keeps more rings open. Vitality is rank against the average page, `PR · N`, on a log scale from .4× (0) to 5× (1).
  - **Links are currents.** Each link pushes water along its chord from source to target, `presence · (30 + 230 · vitality)` CSS px/s², in a Gaussian ribbon of half-width `2.5 + 6.5 · vitality` px, fading over the first and last 18% of the chord. Vitality is the link's flow `d · PR · w / W` against the mean flow `d / links`, log scale .5× to 8×. Rings are drawn out along strong currents into marbled bands and carried into the page they feed.
  - **Teleportation** (1 − d) is a drizzle of tiny clear drops (dispersant): `.6 · (1 − d)` per 24 px cell per second, each opening a pinhole in any ink it lands on; on bare water it leaves nothing, so it adds no texture to the paper.
  - **Per frame:** sources (currents and drops, additive), velocity advection with the currents' push and damping `e^(−t / 1.1 s)`, divergence minus drop injection, 20 Jacobi iterations (pressure 0 beyond the sheet's edge, so water may leave), projection, then ink advection with 4% neighbour spreading, fading `e^(−t / 16 s)`, dripping and drizzle.
  - **Composite:** ink tone-mapped sharply, `.82 · smoothstep(.34, .5, c) + .18 · smoothstep(.04, .3, c)`, so bands keep crisp edges and faint ink is a pale wash; sumi (.075, .072, .07) on plain warm paper (.945, .93, .895), lit faintly by the ink's own slope. The page background is the paper tone; options use dark ink text.
  - **Focus:** a tapped page drips as fast as the richest; currents not touching it are halved.
- **Status:** built and type-checked; lint clean; model and view tests pass. Not yet seen in a browser.
- **Unresolved (to check in the browser):** whether rings stay legible or smear into grey; whether 20 Jacobi iterations resolve the drops' push far enough (rings may only spread a few tens of px); whether currents of 260 px/s² over-stir the water near hubs; whether the pinhole drizzle reads at all; frame cost (about 27 full-screen half-resolution passes per frame).
