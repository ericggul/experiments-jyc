# `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl` — ranked web as one gel (2026-10-06)

- **Question:** does route 7's loop `rank → attention → link weights → rank` read as one living body if pages and links are drawn as a single continuous field, as [`1-glsl`](1-glsl.md) does for route 1?
- **Baseline:** [`/adaptive-coevolving-network/7`](7.md). `model.ts`, `layout.ts`, `views.ts` and both test files are copies of route 7's, with one change: the page cap is 1,000 instead of 300 (2026-10-06, user request; the cap test's growth rate was raised to reach it); every parameter, slider, view, gesture and the 160 presentation-only surfers are route 7's. The renderer `fluid.ts` is a copy of `1-glsl`'s (smooth-union distance field, no outlines, clamped anti-aliasing width, GLSL-reserved names avoided) with two additions: soft cells, and an exponent ceiling that depends on the field's precision.
- **Changed variable:** drawing, scale and play area only.
  - **Pages** are soft cells. Area stays exactly proportional to rank, at 3% of the field (route 7 uses 6%), with a minimum radius of 2.2 px, so pages read as cells joined by links rather than packed discs. The outline carries three shallow lobes (2nd, 3rd and 5th harmonics at 4.5%, 3% and 2% of the radius) drifting at page-specific speeds; they change the area by under .2%. Pages are spaced by 1.12r + 2 px, so they never touch.
  - **Links** follow `1-glsl`'s lesson that node and edge must be one body. A link leaves its source at .92 of the source's radius and enters its target at .85 of the target's, in the same tone, then narrows (`exp(−(s / min(1.9r + 2, 10))^1.4)`) to a middle half-width of `.15 + 30 · d·PR·w/W`, so its width is still the rank it passes on and weak links are hairlines. Weight presence, the 순위 view and focus thin it; it does not breathe, because its width is data.
  - **Grown paths** (user requests: links too straight, then curves too artificial): a link follows a cubic Hermite curve (`tentacle.ts`) that leaves its source and arrives at its target at its own angles (up to .8 rad off the chord, fixed per link by its phase, each drifting ±.22 rad slowly), with tangents .85 of the chord, so every link has a different C or S shape like a neurite. Links longer than 220 px straighten (angles × 220 / length, at least half). A faint 1.2 px ripple travels along it. The ribbon is tessellated along the path in 72 cosine-spaced segments, its normal taken from the path's tangent. Surfers ride the same path, and link taps are hit-tested against it, through the same function in TypeScript. Two other recipes were rejected: a shared sine recipe (bend, twist, travelling wave) read as artificial, and a gentler Hermite base with a two-scale noise meander read as "artificially organic", so the user restored this one.
  - **Growth:** a link's reach along its path is `min(1, 6w)^.6`, so a newly discovered candidate (entering at weight 0) grows out of its source toward its target as its weight rises, and a fading candidate withdraws back into its source; a growing link narrows to a point at its tip. The organism extends and retracts exactly as the model's attention moves.
  - **Depth:** on float32 targets each weight's exponent is capped at 40 (about 22 px of depth) instead of 9.5. Light follows one smooth saturating curve of depth, `.28 + 1.05 (1 − e^(−depth/5))`, so a large page glows from within with no ring. A link's own depth is softly capped (`4 tanh(x/4)`, about 2 px): its edge and its joint with a cell are unchanged, but inside a cell only the cell's depth shapes the light, so roots no longer draw a bright star in the interior (user report: the interior's brightness changed unnaturally and discontinuously).
  - **Tone:** one restrained grey-white (.86, .86, .84) on black. Route 7 has no categories, so colour encodes nothing; hierarchy comes from size, depth and brightness. Surfers are beads of the same tone lit brighter, fused with the link they travel in; a teleport shrinks them away and back. A newborn page and the focused page glow. A link being dragged is a tendril from its page to the pointer. No numbers are drawn; rank shares remain in the screen-reader summary.
  - The layout frame is the whole screen; the options float over it.
- **Options:** route 7's 옵션/닫기 toggle, closed by default; the one-line hint, the 네트워크/순위 views, route 7's four sliders and a 페이지 slider live inside it. The 페이지 slider (20–1,000, step 10, default 100) starts the web over with that many pages, keeping the current d. Route 7's long description paragraph was removed at the user's request (2026-10-06).
- **History (all 2026-10-06):**
  1. First build: links leaving pages at under half their width in a dimmed colour, coral surfers and accents, percentage labels. Not seen in a browser before the next request because the dev server was blocked by unrelated build errors.
  2. At the user's request numbers were removed and pages made "liquid, proliferating": strong lobes plus two buds per page. The user rejected the result as nowhere near `1-glsl`. In Chrome it showed flat grey cotton-ball discs (6% area budget, shading saturated after 5 px), a dark wedge in every page centre (dimmed link colour bleeding in where the exponent cap gave link roots the page's own weight), and thin grey links detached from the pages.
  3. A rank-coloured palette (azure → amber) was tried and rejected by the user at once as childish and kitsch.
  4. Monochrome cells with full-width roots. The user found the links too straight, and the cells' interiors uneven: a two-piece light curve made a ring, and each root, as deep as the cell along its axis, made a bright star inside it.
  5. Tentacle paths from a shared sine recipe, the soft link depth cap and a single light curve. The user found the curves still artificial and not like a growing organism.
  6. Hermite paths with .8 rad angles and links that grow and retract with their weight. The user found the curves somewhat arc-like.
  7. A gentler Hermite base (.45 rad) with a two-scale noise meander. The user found it artificially organic and preferred step 6, which is restored and current.
- **Observed (2026-10-06, user's Mac, Chrome over HTTPS, DPR 2):** renders with no console or WebGL errors. Close-ups show cells shaded smoothly from the centre, with tentacles leaving them without a seam and curving filaments between them; successive screenshots differ, so the tentacles move, but the automation tab was `hidden` and continuous motion was not watched.
- **Budget:** at most 1,000 pages, about 5,000 ribbons and 1,160 cells. Measured in Node on the user's Mac: model .19 / .43 ms and layout .9 / 5.6 ms per frame at 300 / 1,000 pages; the layout's exact O(n²) repulsion and collision dominate at 1,000. DPR up to 2; frames paced to at most 60 Hz on whole vsyncs, three WebGL draw calls; the per-frame link list and instance buffers are reused.
