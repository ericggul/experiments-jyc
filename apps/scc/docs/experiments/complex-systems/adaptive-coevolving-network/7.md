# Route 7: ranked web

Route: `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7`. Date: 2026-10-03. Component: `components/complex-systems/adaptive-coevolving-network/7/` (`RankedWebSeven`).

The user asked for a PageRank study in this family. It had to meet two requirements: the web can be extended, and each page's influence (its rank) is shown directly as its size. The same request removed `/page-rank/4`. Follow-up requests asked for a much denser start (100 pages), a lively, constantly re-evaluated ranking, and continuous rather than discrete change.

## History on 2026-10-03

1. **Discrete version:** 24 pages. New pages linked, and existing pages rewired, to targets ∝ PR + floor/N (Fortunato, Flammini & Menczer 2006), at .4 events per second. Rank barely moved after it settled: the leader was frozen and growth was rich-get-richer only.
2. **100 pages with drifting quality:** discrete rewiring at 6/s. More turnover, but links still appeared and vanished in jumps.
3. **Current, continuous:** described below.

## Model (current)

- **Pages:** 100 to start, at most 300. New pages arrive at .3/s by default.
- **Links:** every page keeps up to 5 candidate links with weights w ≥ 0. The surfer follows link j with probability w_ij / Σ w_i.
- **Rank:** weighted PageRank by warm-started power iteration (L1 tolerance 1e-9), recomputed every frame:
  `PR(i) = (1 − d)/N + d Σ_j PR(j) w_ji / W_j + d Σ_dangling PR(j)/N`, with default d = .85.
- **Attention:** each weight relaxes toward its target's share of appeal,
  `dw_ij/dt = λ (a_j / Σ_{k∈C_i} a_k − w_ij)` with `a_j = (PR_j + floor/N) · e^{q_j}`, λ = .6/s, default floor 3.
- **Quality:** each page has a hidden log quality q that follows an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process (reversion .08/s, default volatility .6/√s). New pages draw q ~ N(0, .6²).
- **Discovery:** at .25/s per page, a page discovers a new candidate drawn ∝ appeal, which enters at weight 0. A full page lets its weakest candidate fade to share 0, and drops it below weight .004.
- **Continuity:** nothing jumps. Links swell and fade, and rank moves at most about .3 percentage points per frame.
- **Boundary:** the drifting quality and continuous attention are this route's extension. The sources are Brin & Page (1998); Fortunato, Flammini & Menczer, PRL 96, 218701 (2006); and Bianconi & Barabási, EPL 54, 436 (2001) for fitness in attachment.

## Screen

- **Pages:** disc area = rank × 6% of the field area (πr² = PR · budget), with a minimum radius of 2.2 px. New pages grow in from nothing. Discs at least 14 px in radius carry their percentage.
- **Links:** an arrow whose width is the rank it actually passes on (d · PR · w / W). Its opacity also follows weight, so links appear and disappear continuously.
- **Surfers:** 160 random surfers, drawn as red dots. They glide along links chosen in proportion to weight, and teleport with probability 1 − d by fading out and back in. They make the definition of PageRank visible. They are presentation only: rank is computed exactly, and surfer density only approximates it.
- **Style:** the family grammar. A white field, ink, and the family red for surfers, focus and new-page rings, with `/2`'s 옵션/닫기 panel and CSS.
- **Views:**
  - *네트워크:* force layout with radius-aware springs and collision. Only links of weight above .08 pull.
  - *순위:* discs in rank order on shared baselines.
  - Switching eases over .9 s.

## Interaction

| Gesture | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Tap empty space | A new page |
| Drag page → page | Add a link at weight .5, which then follows appeal; if the link exists, it fades |
| Drag page → empty space | A new page linked from that page |
| Tap a link | It fades |
| Tap a page | Focus it: its links turn red and its percentage shows |
| Keyboard | N adds a page linked from the leader; Escape clears focus |

The panel holds sliders for 잔잔–요동 (volatility), 고르게–쏠리게 (floor, logarithmic), 새 페이지 없음–많이 (growth), and 순간이동–링크만 (d).

## Checks

- **Tests:**
  - The weighted PageRank equation holds and rank sums to 1, and a cycle ranks every page equally.
  - Continuity: no page moves more than .5 points per frame over 60 s.
  - The leader keeps changing at the default volatility, more than with frozen quality.
  - Attention that follows rank concentrates rank more than even attention.
  - Candidates enter at 0, fade before removal, and stay bounded.
  - A link from the leader lifts a new page more than one from a minor page.
  - Bounds and deterministic replay.
  - The 순위 view's ordering.
- **Cost:** model and layout averaged .11 ms per frame at 100 pages and .6 ms at 300 (worst 2.6 ms) in Node. Canvas drawing has not been checked in a browser.
