# Route 7: echo chambers

Route: `/adaptive-coevolving-network/7`. Date: 2026-10-02. Code: `components/complex-systems/adaptive-coevolving-network/7/`.

## Model card

- **Object and boundary.** A synthetic population of 200 people (cap 400) discussing one issue, from Baumann, Lorenz-Spreen, Sokolov and Starnini, ["Modeling echo chambers and polarization dynamics in social networks"](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.048301), *PRL* 124, 048301 (2020). It is a deliberately bounded abstraction of the paper's model, not an empirical claim about any platform.
- **State.** Each person `i` has a real opinion `x_i` (sign is the side, size is conviction, initially uniform in `[−1, 1]`) and a fixed activity `a_i`, drawn from `F(a) ∝ a^(−γ)` on `[ε, 1]` by inverse CDF. The model also keeps the record of contacts from the last `T = 1.6` model time units. That record is the visible network.
- **Contacts (activity-driven, homophilic).** Time is split into rounds of `h = .1`. At the start of each round, person `i` is active with probability `a_i` and contacts `m` distinct others, choosing `j` with probability `∝ max(|x_i − x_j|, .02)^(−β)`. The active person hears each contact (`A_ij = 1`), and with probability `r` the contact also hears them (`A_ji = 1`). These contacts hold for the round.
- **Opinions.** Within a round, Euler steps of at most `.02` integrate `dx_i/dt = −x_i + K Σ_j A_ij(t) tanh(α x_j)`. A person held by the participant is skipped.
- **Parameters and defaults.** `m = 4`, `r = .5`, `ε = .01`, `γ = 2.1` (mean activity ≈ .041), `K = 7.5`, controversy `α = 3` (slider 0–4), homophily `β = 3` (slider 0–4).
- **Departure from the paper.** The paper uses `N = 1000`, `m = 10`, `K = 3` and redraws contacts every `dt = .01`. Here `m = 4` and `h = .1`, so that individual conversations stay countable on screen (about 330 contacts per model unit, 200 per second). `K` was raised to 7.5 to keep the mean coupling `K·m·⟨a⟩·(1 + r) ≈ 1.84`, the same as in the paper. The price is shot noise: each heard contact moves a quiet person by up to `K·h = .75`.
- **Direction convention.** Here the active person is the listener and reciprocity makes the contact listen too. With `r = .5` this only decides which side gets the reciprocal half. It has not been checked line by line against the paper's text.
- **Macro observables** (`measureEcho`): alignment `|Σx| / Σ|x|`; mean conviction `⟨|x|⟩`; the share leaning right; the share of recent contacts that cross sides; and echo, the Pearson correlation between `x_i` and the mean opinion of `i`'s recent contacts.

## Measured pure-model results

These runs use 200 people, the default seed `0x5bd1e995`, `K = 7.5` and `t = 30` model units (50 s on screen).

| α | β | alignment | conviction | crossing contacts | echo | regime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| .05 | 3 | — | .00 | — | — | neutral consensus |
| .3 | 0 | 1.00 | .00 | 0 | — | consensus (still collapsing) |
| .4 | 0 | 1.00 | .56 | 0 | −.10 | weak radicalization |
| 3 | 0 | 1.00 | 1.73 | 0 | −.32 | radicalization |
| 3 | 1 | 1.00 | 1.72 | 0 | −.16 | radicalization |
| 3 | 2 | .40 | 1.48 | .08 | .42 | polarization |
| 3 | 3 | .05 | 1.54 | .04 | .55 | polarization with echo chambers |
| 1 | 3 | .42 | 1.09 | .11 | .56 | polarization |
| .5 | 3 | .41 | .70 | .12 | .60 | polarization |

- **Thresholds.** For consensus versus radicalization, the critical `α` is between .3 and .4 at `β = 0`. This matches mean-field `α_c ≈ 1/1.84 ≈ .54` within the noise. Over seeds 1–3, `β = 1` sometimes stays split until `t ≈ 20` before one side wins. At `β ≥ 2` the population stays split, with camp shares between .3 and .7.
- **Timing.** Regimes are established by `t ≈ 10` (about 17 s on screen).
- **Activity and extremity.** As in the paper, the most active people hold the most extreme opinions. In a polarized run, people with activity `.65` and `.51` reached `|x| ≈ 14` and `11`, while the median `|x|` was about .6.
- **Hysteresis.** At `t = 20` with `β = 3`, setting `β = 0` turned two camps into one side within 10 units (alignment .02 → 1.00). Returning to `β = 3` keeps one side, because homophily cannot make a second side from nothing. Raising `α` from a consensus at `α = .05` gives radicalization even when `β = 3`, because the opinion differences that homophily needs have decayed.
- **Seeding a camp.** Start with a population radicalized at `β = 0` (`t = 15`, seeds 1–4) and add `k` people at `x = ∓2` with activity 1 on the empty side, then run 30 units. The new side's share was:
  - `k = 1`: 0 at `β ≤ 2`; one seed in four reached .59 at `β = 3`;
  - `k = 3`: 0 at `β ≤ 1`; .62–.67 at `β = 3`;
  - `k = 5`: .40–.68 at `β = 2`; .65–.72 at `β = 3`.

  Talkative people listen to the closest-minded members of the majority, convert them with reciprocal contacts, and the new camp then protects itself as an echo chamber. Without homophily they are absorbed.
- **Dragging across.** A person was moved from the negative camp to `x = +1.5`, held for 1 unit, then released. They stayed positive in 26 of 30 cases at `β = 3`, against 17 of 30 at `β = .5`.

## Screen encoding

- White field. Dot colour runs from ink near `x = 0` to blue for negative and red for positive, reaching full colour at `|x| = 1.5`. Dot size grows with `√a`.
- **Recent contacts.** Each contact is a quadratic arc bowed upward, drawn out from the person who reached out over about .18 s. Its alpha falls as `(1 − age/T)^1.5`, and mutual contacts are thicker. Arcs within a side take that side's colour; arcs across sides are ink. An echo chamber therefore shows as two colours with almost no ink arcs. The network is literally the recent conversation history.
- **Marks.** A newly added person shows a ring that fades in .9 s. A held person carries a thin ink ring.
- **Text.** No title, counters or legend. The only canvas text is one label in the opinion view (`말이 많을수록 위`).
- **Views** (presentation only; the model never reads them):
  - *의견* (default): opinion across, compressed by `tanh(|x|/2.5)` so the heavy tail fits. Activity runs up on a log scale with a fixed jitter of ±.05. A faint vertical line marks zero.
  - *대화망*: a force layout whose springs are the recent contacts, weighted by freshness, with O(n²) repulsion and gravity. At 200 people and `β = 3`, the two camps' centroids were 118 px apart against a within-camp spread of about 77 px (390×640 frame, 40 s).
  - *분포*: the same opinion axis as stacked dots, one column per dot-width bin, ordered by id. Contacts are shown at 40%.

  A view switch eases from a snapshot to the live targets over .9 s (cubic in-out), then follows at rate 12/s, as in route 2.

## Interactions

- **Drag a person sideways.** This holds their opinion: horizontal travel maps through the axis, so in the opinion and distribution views they follow the pointer. Their arcs re-target at once to people near the new opinion. Releasing hands them back to the dynamics.
- **Tap empty space.** This adds a person with activity 1, at the tapped opinion in the opinion and distribution views, or at the opinion of the nearest person in the conversation view.
- **Keyboard.** On the focused canvas, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight add such a person at `x = ∓2`.
- **Options.** The bottom 옵션/닫기 toggle opens a Korean model description, the view buttons, and two sliders: 온건–논쟁 (`α`) and 아무나–비슷한 사람만 (`β`).
- **Other behaviour.** A hint shows until the first interaction. A screen-reader summary of side shares, mean conviction and crossing contacts updates every 2 s. Tempo is .6 model units per second. Under reduced motion, tempo drops to .3 and there are no rings or draw-out.

## Tests

`model.test.ts` covers:

- deterministic replay;
- the activity range, and that an active person makes `m` distinct contacts;
- consensus at `α = .05`;
- radicalization at `β = 0`;
- polarization at `β = 3`, with alignment < .6, crossing contacts < .12 and echo > .3, compared with alignment > .98 at `β = 0` on the same seed;
- dropping `β` merges the camps;
- three talkers grow a camp at `β = 3` but not at `β = 0` (seeds 1–3);
- hold and release;
- the population cap.

`views.test.ts` covers:

- the axis mapping round trip;
- sides separating at the centre in the opinion and distribution views;
- all views staying inside the frame at desktop and phone size;
- the opinion view placing activity upward.

## Unresolved

- The listener/speaker direction convention: see the model card.
- Whether shot noise from `h = .1` and `m = 4` hides structure on phones, and whether a smaller `h` with fainter arcs reads better.
- The conversation-view separation is moderate. Quiet people have few springs and float between the camps.
- Neutral consensus is visually just a collapse to the centre line, and from there homophily cannot produce polarization without participant seeding. This matches the model, but it should be watched in use.
