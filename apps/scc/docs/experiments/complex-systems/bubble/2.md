# `/bubble/2` — `/1` with its network and visual parameters open (2026-10-08)

- **Baseline:** a standalone copy of [`/1`](1.md) (7-glsl-6): route 7's adaptive PageRank web, the soap-bubble raft
  renderer and layout, unchanged.
- **Changed:** the controls only. Two round triggers at the bottom right, in the grammar of
  `/transportation/road-signs/direction/4`:
  - **Network** — the web's own parameters: 네트워크/순위 view, quality volatility, rank concentration (appeal floor),
    new pages per second, the probability d of following a link, and the page count (restarts the web).
  - **Visual** — the renderer's main constants, opened as uniforms, plus the raft's scale and flow: bubble area
    (share of the screen), threshold for drawn flow portions, film thickness, interference colour, film current speed,
    swirl, dome depth, grazing-angle reflection, wall brightness, reflection strength (was window reflection), ambient, exposure. Defaults are
    `/1`'s values exactly; "bubble/1 값으로" restores them.
  - **Reflected surroundings** (visual panel, added 2026-10-08): 창 (`/1`'s window, the default), 흐린 하늘, 스튜디오,
    밤 도시, 나뭇잎 빛, 물빛, or an uploaded image wrapped round as a panorama. Every mode except 창 reflects a direction
    computed from the film normal, weighted by Fresnel and by 반사 세기.
  - **Palette:** colour pickers for 빛 (light reflected and lighting the film), 막 (film tint) and 배경 (what lies
    behind; it shows faintly through the films), and presets 무채 (`/1`), 새벽, 호박, 이끼, 종이. White, white and black
    reproduce `/1`.
  - **Reflection options** (all 0 by default, which is `/1`): 반사 곡면 blends `/1`'s shallow cap into a full sphere
    over each bubble's disc (held to the cap within a few px of a wall), so one bubble holds the whole surroundings
    like a fisheye; 환경 선명도·대비 narrows the surroundings' soft edges, widens their range and rolls highlights off;
    반사에 간섭색 입히기 colours the reflection by the film's interference instead of laying it over the film;
    뒷면 반사 adds the far inside face mirroring the scene upside down. Uploaded images are sampled with seam-free,
    sharpened derivatives. Studio, sphere 1, contrast 0.8: 6.4 / 15 ms, 60 fps (DPR 2, 1470 × 706).
- **Purpose:** to move back and forth between network behaviour and visual parameters and find, by adjustment, what a
  better bubble rendering of an evolving network needs — instead of rebuilding the substrate.
