## Concept

The Goldfishes proposal (`~/Documents/EMERGENCE/output/pdf/Goldfishes_final.pdf`): in the information overflow we
lose long-term vision, blinded by what is currently trending; if the newest trend is AI, every system chases it,
the hype circulates into related keywords, and our attention becomes a goldfish's — short, reactive, unaware where
it leads; "every corner becomes stimulus". `/2` already had the goldfish as PageRank's random surfer. Here the
pages themselves become media consumers: each bubble is a monitor (Paik's *TV Garden*, which the proposal cites)
and what it shows is a copy of what the bigger pages it links to are showing.

References, each read as a translation rather than a look to imitate: [Refik Anadol, *Unsupervised — Machine
Hallucinations*](https://moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5535) (a collection morphing without rest — here the
cross-fade of 제각각 and 유행, but with a visible cause in the graph instead of a latent walk); [Hito Steyerl, *In
Defense of the Poor Image*](https://www.e-flux.com/journal/10/61362/in-defense-of-the-poor-image/) (images get
poorer as they circulate — the 256 px tiles, the small bubbles' mip levels, the portions that carry a page's image
along a link); [Ryoji Ikeda, *data-verse*](https://www.audemarspiguet.com/en/news/art/ryoji-ikeda-data-verse-trilogy)
and [Julian Stallabrass, *Sublime Calculation*](https://newleftreview.org/issues/ii132/articles/julian-stallabrass-sublime-calculation)
(the data sublime: more points than can be taken in, with a temporary order still readable — here which bubble
follows which trend); [Tabor Robak](https://archive2013-2020.ctm-festival.de/archive/all-artists/p-t/tabor-robak/)
(hyper-synthetic renders of over-stimulation — the tech-keyword set's own texture).

Rebuilt 2026-10-12 after the user saw it as a picture inside a bubble, a layer apart from it, and asked for emoji = ball = the whole layer: the glyph now fills its tile edge to edge, and with this surface (`mediaSolid`) each bubble cell *is* the emoji — an opaque ball with the glyph laid straight onto it, shown as drawn — the first build lit it by the film's window (highlight, rim light, reflected surroundings); the user found that glow wrong on a picture, it belongs to the film only, so only a crease where cells press together remains, tilting a little in the picture plane with 흐름 (a turn in depth slid the face off one side and showed a black crescent). No film lies over it; 입히기 and 막 비침 are hidden for it. With 닿을 때만 a bubble is film until a fish touches it and then becomes the emoji. Round faces only (user, 2026-10-12; 49, objects and faces with hands, hats or steam outside the circle left out): the circle all Apple faces share is measured once from 🙂's drawn pixels (`measureText` reports the em box, ~12 % larger than the ink, which left a black ring) and scaled to the tile, so the face's outline is the bubble's rim; a tongue or tear past it is cut. The first build (one glyph at 62 % of the tile, wrapped by 구면 under the film) is gone.
- **Texture** (`foam.ts`): a `TEXTURE_2D_ARRAY` of 256 × 256 RGBA8 layers (at most 320), mipmapped, anisotropy 4,
  mirrored across its sides. Tiles are decoded four at a time (`media/loader.ts`: fetch → `createImageBitmap` →
  cover crop on a reused canvas → `texSubImage3D`), mipmaps rebuilt every twelve tiles, decoded sources cached so a
  surface change never re-fetches. Per bubble a second data texture carries layer, next layer, cross-fade and a
  phase; portions carry their source page's (흐름 버블에도, default on).
- **Mapping** (입히기): 평면 (the image across the disc), 조각 (a window of 30 % of the image placed by the bubble's
  phase, so neighbours sharing an image show different pieces), 구면 (default: the image wrapped on a ball, its visible
  half across the bubble, compressed at the rim, rocking ±20° about its axis so nothing mirrors or seams), 결 (a
  band 3 % of the image's height stretched over the bubble — vertical streaks, the pillars' side faces — sweeping
  down the image and back). The picture is seen *through* the film: multiplied by `1 − fresnel · 막 비침`, with the
  film's own light (interference, Fresnel, walls, window) laid over it. 채도 0.8, 밝기, 확대, 흐름.
- **보임** (added 2026-10-11): 항상 (default since 2026-10-12), or 금붕어가 닿을 때만 (the default before) — a page's picture comes while a fish is in contact
  with it and goes when none is, each over 바뀜 부드럽게 (the media fade, default 0.15 s since 2026-10-12, first 0.25 s; 0 is instant — the user
  first asked for no animation, then for it to align with that value). Portions carry their source's state. Without
  fish nothing shows.
- **Regimes** (바뀜, `media/model.ts`, pure): 고정 (a page keeps the image it was born with, from its index); 제각각 (the pillars' playback: interval `1 / (rate · (1 ± 0.72 r))`, always another image, cross-fade
  0.15 s, 초당 바뀜 수 default 2); 유행 따라가기 (default, user 2026-10-11) — every 새 유행 주기 (6 s, jittered ±30 %) each group's leading
  page takes up a new image, and whenever a page's portion of rank is taken in by the page it links to, the sender
  copies what the receiver is turning into with probability 따라갈 확률 (0.6) scaled by how far the receiver outranks
  it (nothing between equals, the full probability at twice the rank). So pages imitate upward: a trend runs from
  hubs down the links, hubs keep theirs until the next, and disconnected groups chase different ones. 싫증 (off) makes
  a page that has held one image that long look elsewhere.

## Seen in the browser (2026-10-11, hidden tab driven by the MessageChannel shim so the web keeps evolving, 1470 × 706, DPR 2)

- 섞음 · 구면 · 고정: a dense raft of faces, cats, kisses, dark tech renders and grey words, each held under its film
  with `/2`'s highlights and walls intact; the sphere mapping magnifies the middle of each image and compresses the
  rim, so bubbles read as balls holding pictures rather than as badges. 평면 reads flatter, like a photo collage;
  조각 breaks faces into fragments; 결 is the pillars' streaked material and the most "bubble"-like of the four, at
  the cost of the content's legibility (Steyerl's poor image).
- 그룹 4 · 유행 따라가기: within about 20 s each group had converged on its own image (one all "VR", one "OOP", one
  a chip render, one a face), 20 s later each had moved on, lagging followers still holding the previous trend
  beside the new — the proposal's hype cycle, legible per echo chamber. With one group the whole raft follows one
  leader; 싫증 breaks the monoculture.
- White keyword tiles at first shouted over the photographs; they are now pale grey at half the tile's width.

## 기술 surface: regenerated (2026-10-11)

The user rejected the generated keyword atlas ("너무 구리다": glossy stock-render clichés) and asked for a
"sophisticated" set, "not too cheesy, not too explicit". The OpenAI Images API route
(`media/sources/generate-tech-keywords.mjs`, whose STYLE and 36 scenes were rewritten for this) was refused —
the key's organisation has no credits (`credit_balance_exhausted`) — so the set was made by the user's Codex
agent (`gpt-6-astra`, its built-in image generation under the ChatGPT login) from the same prompts: a quiet
fine-art / editorial photograph per keyword, medium format, soft daylight, greys and pale earth tones, one clear
subject, no text, logos, neon, circuitry, robots, faces. Each keyword is translated obliquely — AI a starling
murmuration, ML wind ripples in sand, DL the shaft of a stone well, GPU an aerial of greenhouse roofs, UI a row of
worn enamel buttons, UX a desire path across a lawn, HCI the bright patch worn into a door handle, QA one cracked
cup in a row of identical ones, NFT an empty wall with a picture hook and the pale rectangle a painting left,
DAO a circle of empty folding chairs. Six were regenerated after review (LLM a bust's face, RAG box labels, SDK
and IDE ruler numerals, DNS pigeon-hole marks, NFT too faint for a small disc). 36 JPEGs, 1024², ~12 MB, in
`public/images/1011/tech-keywords/` with `ledger.json` (prompt per image; the tool reports no model name).
`catalogue.ts`'s 기술 set now points at them; the old atlas copy under `public/images/0908/tech-keyword-atlas`
is no longer referenced by `/3`. Under the film the set reads as pale architecture and material — quieter than
the photographs of faces and cats, and much quieter than the renders it replaces.

## 기술 surface: marks on balls (2026-10-12)

The user rejected the regenerated photographs too ("다 개쓰레기": they do not represent the tech industry at all) and
asked for each keyword represented as a ball, as the emoji are. Each keyword is now the mark of the company or product
people mean by it (`media/tech-marks.ts`, simple-icons 16.25.0 paths and brand colours; OpenAI's from Goldfishes'
`company-logos/openai.svg`; Windows' four panes): AI Gemini, ML TensorFlow, DL PyTorch, AGI DeepMind, GPT OpenAI, LLM
Claude, NLP Hugging Face, RAG LangChain, CPU Intel, GPU NVIDIA, NPU Qualcomm, RAM Samsung, OS Linux, PC Windows, IoT
Raspberry Pi, AR Snapchat, VR Meta, XR Apple, UI Figma, UX Sketch, HCI ACM, MVP Y Combinator, API Stripe, SDK Android,
IDE VSCodium, OOP C++, QA Selenium, DB PostgreSQL, SQL MySQL, CDN Cloudflare, DNS Namecheap, URL Chrome, VPN NordVPN,
VM VMware, NFT OpenSea, DAO Ethereum. Each tile is a ball of the brand colour with the mark at half its width (white;
near-black on pale colours; a near-black brand becomes a pale ball with a dark mark) and the ball's own volume drawn in
(paler upper left, darker rim, no highlight). Like 이모지 it is a solid surface: the bubble is the ball, no film over it.
In 섞음 the marks sit under the film like the other pictures. The photographs stay in `public/images/1011/tech-keywords`,
unreferenced. In the browser the raft reads at once as the industry's app-icon landscape; wordmarks (VMware, Samsung,
MySQL) are the weakest at small sizes.

**기술-버블** (added 2026-10-12, beside 기술, which stays as it is): the user asked for the marks with more of a
bubble's feel. The same 36 marks, not solid: each tile is drawn for the film to lie over — black (clear, as the picture
is added to the film) in the middle, the brand colour gathering towards the rim as a thick film's colour does (0.08 at
the centre, 0.75 at the rim), the mark at 46 % in a pale version of the colour (a near-black brand is a grey bubble).
Under the film's interference, highlights and walls it reads as a raft of tinted soap bubbles each holding a logo; the
middle stays clear, so neighbours and the background show through. Not in 섞음.

**FOMO 글자 색** (added 2026-10-12, a choice inside FOMO's text controls, not another surface): 흰색 (default, as
before) or 브랜드 — each phrase takes one of the marks' brand colours in turn and is drawn in 기술-버블's way: the tint
gathering at the rim, the words in a pale version of the colour (글자 불투명도 scales them; 0.7 is the marks' strength).
In the browser the FOMO raft reads as 기술-버블's tinted bubbles with phrases in place of logos.

## Tried and withdrawn (2026-10-11)

- **Touch glow:** a bubble a goldfish touched brightened for about half a second (film body ×2.5 at full glow, a
  little fill light, from the school's contact time per page, cooling exponentially). The user asked for it to be
  removed after seeing it; the code is gone, the slider with it.
- **Bright pictures:** turning 밝기 up flattened highlights into clipped blocks and made the 256 px tiles' softness
  obvious on hub bubbles. Now the picture rolls off into white above 0.75 (`softClip`), and surfaces of at most 100
  layers (키워드, 기술, 고양이, 정치인, 테크 얼굴) get 512 px tiles; 섞음 keeps 256 px for memory.

- **Forum-slang FOMO** (2026-10-12): after a note that the first FOMO set was too abstract, it was replaced by 48
  terms from crypto Twitter, Blind, LinkedIn and AI Twitter (HODL, WAGMI, NGMI, TC or GTFO, Feel the AGI, Copium …).
  The user found the acronyms unwanted and the selection itself read as machine-curated, and preferred the first set;
  it is back, with its own acronyms written out (State of the Art, Raised 100 Million, Artificial General Intelligence).
