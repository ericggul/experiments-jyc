# `/bubble/2` — a random Apollonian network grown as bubbles born in triple junctions (2026-10-07, browser pending)

- **Question:** can a growing scale-free network be a foam in which nothing is drawn as a link? A [random Apollonian network](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.71.046141) (Zhou, Yan & Wang, PRE 71, 046141 (2005); [Andrade et al., PRL 94, 018702 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.018702)) is planar, scale-free and small-world: a triangular face is picked uniformly and a node is inserted into it, linked to its three corners. In a foam a triangular face of the contact graph *is* a triple junction (Plateau border), so a bubble is born there, and its three walls are its three links.
- **Baseline:** [`/bubble/1`](../1.md) (= 7-glsl-6). Its walls came from overlapping discs, not links; its film was stateless; its 1.4 px `edgeOn` line flickered (20.3/255 frame difference); it filled the middle third of the screen.
- **Files:** `components/complex-systems/bubble/2/` — `model.ts` (network), `layout.ts` (geometry), `frame.ts` (renderer buffers), `foam.ts` (WebGL2), `index.tsx`, tests `model.test.ts`, `layout.test.ts`.

## Changed variables

1. **Network (`model.ts`, pure, seeded mulberry32).** A triangulation of the torus (seed: 6 × 4 grid with one diagonal, every bubble six links; 4 × 6 on portrait screens), so the foam has no outer face and fills any frame. Faces keep stable ids.
   - Births: Poisson arrivals at `growth` (default 2.5 /s) × clamp(1 + 3(1 − n/target), 0, 4), each into a uniformly random face not touching a rupture. A bubble with k links touches k of the 2N faces: linear preferential attachment.
   - Film: thickness h = 1 at birth, drains as dh/dt = −h · (n/target)² / (τ · pace · (size/mean size)), with pace ∈ [0.5, 1.5] per bubble. Larger films drain slower (Reynolds thinning, softened to the first power); a crowded foam drains faster. τ = (1.6 · target / growth − 1/1.4 − 0.7) / ln 25 (the 1.6 is a Node calibration).
   - Rupture: once h < 0.04 (black film), hazard 1.4 /s. The wall to the neighbour with most links (among those allowed by the link condition, not busy, not just merged) opens; 0.7 s later the two coalesce, which is an edge contraction (film thickness becomes the size-weighted mean). Contraction is allowed only when the two share exactly two neighbours, so the result is again a triangulation.
   - Cascade: when a rupture starts, each other neighbour with h < 0.07 ruptures with probability 0.3, after 0.14–0.5 s.
   - Size rule: linear size (k − 2)^0.8, area ∝ (k − 2)^1.6. [Lewis's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis%27s_law) (area ∝ k − 2) would give 0.5 and Feltham's law (diameter ∝ k − 2) 1. A hub needs perimeter for k walls, so the exponent lies between the two. Merge-target policies measured: "most links" keeps the heaviest tail. Smallest-degree gave a CCDF slope of −2.4 and max degree ~40; random gave −2.2.
2. **Geometry (`layout.ts`): a 2D dry-foam vertex model** ([Kawasaki, Nagai & Nakashima, Phil. Mag. B 60, 399 (1989)](https://doi.org/10.1080/13642818908205916)). The degrees of freedom are the junctions, one per face. A bubble is the polygon of its junction ring, so a wall exists for each link and for nothing else, by construction.
   - Forces: wall tension 1, and ideal-gas pressure 40 · (A₀/A − 1)/√A₀. Mobility is 0.55 × the present size of the smallest bubble at the junction, with steps clamped to 0.15 × that size; 2 substeps per frame.
   - Wall limits: walls shorter than 0.1 × the smaller bubble's size are pushed open, and walls longer than 0.28 × the shorter frame side stiffen ×12 (the torus needs walls under half a period).
   - Targets: area targets scale to tile the frame, capped at 7 % of the frame.
   - Birth: the parent junction splits into three junctions 0.6 px along its three walls, and the new bubble's target grows in over 1.8 s (an inverse T2 process).
   - Rupture: the opening wall loses tension and the pair shares one pressure, so the corners at its ends straighten before the contraction removes them.
3. **Renderer (`foam.ts`), WebGL2, 2 full-screen-resolution passes at DPR ≤ 2.**
   - Pass 1 draws every bubble as a triangle fan from its first junction, adding ±(1, id) by facing into an RGBA16F target. The winding number makes G/R the owner id for any simple polygon, convex or not. Up to ~6.8k vertices.
   - Pass 2 walks the owner's junction ring (texture, ≤ 192 entries per pixel, ~2.5k entries total). It computes the exact distance to the owner's walls and rounds convex corners by their junction's Plateau-border radius.
   - Border radius: wet × 0.32 × the smallest bubble's size, ×(0.75 + 0.5 · thickest film), ≤ 0.45 × the adjacent walls. It follows its target at 2.2 /s, and new junctions inherit their parent's, so a bud starts inside the swollen border and emerges from it.
   - Walls: liquid 1.1 CSS px wide, box-filtered over the pixel footprint (the cure for bubble/1's sub-pixel line flicker).
   - Film: band-averaged two-beam interference over 400–490/490–580/580–700 nm, so thick film goes grey and black film reflects nothing. Colour is tempered to 22 %. Mean thickness is 14 → 1500 nm by h^1.3; the crown drains first, slow domain-warped currents fold it, and the pattern cross-fades to the survivor's during a rupture. A shallow cap gives Fresnel, a soft environment and one window highlight. The rupturing wall thins away over 0.25 s.
4. **UI:** bubble/1's grammar (`옵션`/`닫기`). The panel holds a one-line hint and three sliders: births (0.5–10 /s), dry↔wet (0.05–1, default 0.45) and bubble count target (120–900, default 420; it shifts the steady state without restarting). Tapping adds a bubble at the nearest junction and N adds one beside the bubble with most links. A screen-reader summary updates every 2 s. Reduced motion runs the foam at 0.25 × pace and freezes the film currents. Frames are paced to ≤ 60 Hz on whole vsyncs.

## Measured in Node (2026-10-07)

- Pure RAN on the torus, N = 420: max degree 42, degree CCDF slope −1.69 for k ≥ 6. At N = 1000: max 69, slope −1.68, mean degree 6.000 (the asymptotic slope is −2).
- Steady state, default parameters (300–900 s of one seed):
  - Count: mean 392 (min 348, max 429) for a target of 420.
  - Max degree: mean 45, max 63; CCDF slope −1.68; top tenth of bubbles hold 35 % of walls.
  - Ruptures: 2,275 ruptures, 2,274 merges, 0 heals.
  - Cascades: 292 of more than one burst, 17 above 10, 1 above 30; the largest had 40.
- Two earlier bugs, now fixed:
  - A cascade threshold of 0.16/0.45 percolated, with cascades up to 291 bursts.
  - A fractional birth accumulator fired ~3× the set rate.
- Geometry over 60–120 s, 1470 × 762:
  - Area error 3 %, no inverted bubbles past their first frames. Sub-pixel buds can flip for ≤ 6 frames inside their border.
  - Shortest wall 0.07 × the smaller bubble's size; largest bubble 5.4 % of the frame.
  - Fold check: 88 fan triangles from the centroid fold. Hubs are not star-shaped, which is why the renderer uses winding instead of fans from centres.
- CPU per frame: model 0.024 ms, layout 0.27 ms, buffers 0.09 ms.
- Failed geometry attempts:
  - Disc springs at the contact distance with apex repulsion and face-area barriers tangled: 300+ inverted faces, and only 37 % of links became walls.
  - A periodic Tutte embedding ([Gortler, Gotsman & Thurston, CAGD 23, 83 (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cagd.2005.05.002)) had no folds, but faces went to 0.04 px² (Apollonian degeneration) and weight control could not hold the sizes.
  - Degree-set radii cannot be realised by disc geometry around RAN hubs.
- Tests: `node --test model.test.ts layout.test.ts` check:
  - torus invariants (V − E + F = 0, every edge in two faces), RAN tail, determinism and the steady state;
  - one ring entry per link, one wall per link and no inversions;
  - that the polygons tile the frame exactly.

## Round 2 (2026-10-08, rebuilt by the lead): an Apollonian packing as a hyperreal bubble raft

- **Network:** random Apollonian network built as its own geometry — a random Apollonian packing inside a rim. A bubble
  nucleates in a gap where three bubbles meet with the size and place that touch all three (complex Descartes theorem),
  so a wall is always a link. Old bubbles with only their first three links coalesce into their largest neighbour
  (wall ruptures, gas pours across) and reopen their gap. Gas moves along links toward larger neighbours with even
  re-supply (PageRank as Ostwald ripening); a wall's film streams with the gas crossing it.
- **Rendering (`optics.ts`):** transparent 3D films on dark liquid under a perspective camera. Domes (bubble spheres with
  centres just below the liquid) and walls (the surfaces f_i = f_j) are impostors that solve their own ray hit and add
  their reflections into one RGBA16F target; the liquid adds its Fresnel reflection. Thin-film interference: two-beam
  Airy reflectance, both polarisations, nine wavelengths through CIE 1931 to sRGB. Studio: small overhead panel, low back
  strip, soft side strip, dim room. Each bubble's touching neighbours are sorted by azimuth so a pixel searches only the
  walls at its own azimuth (binary search) — this took the frame from 19.5 to 6.6 ms.
- **Failed on the way (same day):** a 3D Apollonian cluster of 220 transparent bubbles stacked dozens of films per pixel
  (unreadable, 20 ms) and small hard lights made rainbow squares; random seeding plus springs could not realise the planar
  contacts (587 of 774 links not touching, 269 unlinked overlaps) — hence the exact packing.
- **Measured (Node):** 356 bubbles at saturation (gaps under 0.55% of the rim stay empty), 0 links without contact, 0 unlinked
  overlaps after 120 s of turnover (27 births, 27 coalescences); largest degrees 91, 81, 72; model step median 0.01 ms.
- **Measured (browser, 2940 × 1412, 30 s, frame shim):** cost median 6.6 ms, p95 12.5 ms, max 32 ms; flicker 0.42; blinks 48.
- **Unresolved:** p95 above the 8 ms target; whether gas flux on walls reads at all; the raft is mostly very dark between
  reflections; awaiting the user's verdict.
- **Verdict (user, 2026-10-08):** rejected — cheesy, not high quality, not the bubble visual wanted; the photographic
  "optics" framing (perspective camera over liquid, studio reflections, depth of field-like focus) was never requested.
  Lesson: the lead again substituted its own visual direction for the user's; the next step is a concrete visual
  reference from the user before any further build.

## Round 3 (2026-10-08, in progress): living films, from references

The user's direction, restated: 2D and top-down like `/1`, but a different visual approach from `/1`, and of higher
quality. `/1` paints a procedural noise film on each bubble. Round 3 simulates the film instead.

- **References (searched 2026-10-08).**
  - Visual:
    - [Nicky Assmann, *Solace*](https://vimeo.com/21168008): a monumental soap film whose iridescent colour moves as
      turbulent flow until it bursts.
    - [Kym Cox, life cycle of soap-bubble iridescence](https://www.cupoty.com/kym-cox-life-cycle-of-soap-bubble-iridescence):
      colour comes from a film that "continually flows, swirls and drains", in a cycle of a few seconds.
    - [Fabian Oefner, *Iridient*](https://petapixel.com/2013/07/01/photographer-captures-soap-bubbles-as-they-burst-and-disappear/):
      the burst.
  - Physics and method:
    - [Ishida et al., *A Model for Soap Film Dynamics with Evolving Thickness*, SIGGRAPH 2020](https://visualcomputing.ist.ac.at/publications/2020/AMFSFDWET/):
      thickness as state, carried by the film's flow, draining and rupturing.
    - [Gu et al. 2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cav.2289): real-time thin-film thickness by shallow-water
      equations, with precomputed interference reflectance.
    - [Couder's soap-film two-dimensional turbulence](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/terra-bulla-the-influence-of-yves-couder-on-the-emerging-domain-o/).
    - [Marginal regeneration](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.07966): thin film rising from the Plateau border.
  - Network:
    - [*Planar network statistics for two-dimensional rupturing foams* (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20858): a foam
      between plates treated as a planar network whose ruptures change Lewis and Aboav statistics.
- **Design (`film.ts`).**
  - The packing (round 2's model) gives the bubbles. Ownership comes from `f_i`, resolved exactly per pixel from a
    half-resolution owner pass.
  - The film's velocity and thickness run at half CSS resolution, advected within their own bubble:
    - plumes of thin film are pulled from every wall, stronger in a bubble gaining gas through its links;
    - a weak curl stirs the film;
    - thickness relaxes toward the bubble's level (thick when young, draining with age), and black film spreads before
      coalescence.
  - The composite shades thickness by nine-wavelength Airy interference at a shallow dome's incidence, with thin liquid
    borders at the walls.
- **Status:** built; typecheck and lint pass. Not yet seen in the browser: the Chrome extension disconnected.
- **Verdict on round 3 (user, 2026-10-08):** rejected — "an obviously comic 2D visual". Flat discs filled edge to edge with
  saturated interference colour and dark borders have no volume and no transparency, so they do not read as bubbles.
  A bubble is a transparent sphere of film: mostly see-through, with light only at Fresnel rims, at the reflections of
  its front and back surfaces, and in faint flowing interference.
- **Round 4 (orthographic ray-traced spheres in an Apollonian disc), rejected (user, 2026-10-08):** cheesy, colourful,
  flat texture that reads as computational AI slop, not attractive — every item on the list the user had asked to avoid.
  The method itself made perfect tangent circles (rule 7: never uniform circles, never 2D-graphic), and physically
  saturated interference reads as stock "soap bubble" imagery.

## Round 5 (2026-10-08): pressed wet foam from photographs

- **Looked at first:** about 60 macro photographs of foam and bubble rafts (Google Images: soap foam on black, bubble rafts,
  Plateau borders). Videos could not play in the hidden automation tab. What real foam shows: cells never overlap and press
  into curved polygons; the light lives in the liquid (glassy Plateau borders, concave fillets where three cells meet); cells
  are near-black with one soft window reflection that bends with each dome; near-monochrome silver.
- **Network:** random Apollonian packing with a free, growing edge (new bubbles also settle against two edge bubbles); a
  rim-face bug that picked the outer Descartes circle (radius 1.48, 66 overlaps) was fixed. Node: 0 overlaps, 0 untouched
  links after 180 s (178 births, 183 coalescences).
- **Rendering (`lace.ts`):** circles drawn 14% larger so neighbours press into walls (owner by f_i). Each cell is its
  polygon with corners rounded at the Plateau-border radius (rounded SDF intersection of its two nearest constraints), so
  walls are thin lamellae and junctions are concave triangles by geometry. A height field — film domes, concave liquid
  menisci — gives normals that reflect one dark room (a soft key light, a dim opposite light); water by Fresnel plus light
  scattered in curved liquid; film by two-face Fresnel. Four rotated-grid samples per device pixel.
- **Measured (2940 × 1412):** cost median 4.5 ms, p95 11.2 ms; flicker 0.49; blinks 71.
- **Rejected on the way:** flat grey strokes for borders (looked like a 2D diagram); smooth-min liquid width (uniform lines,
  mushy joins); stamped rectangular window highlights.
- **Round 5, second pass (user: "better than before, but comic; make it look like real bubbles, not computer graphics"; "no
  cheap glow or edge treatment").** Added only physical light: a room with a mullioned window, a soft ceiling and pale walls
  (steep liquid and film edges reflect the walls by Fresnel — no drawn glow or outline); a faint ripple on films and liquid;
  a faint pastel interference tint on films; a deeper, larger foam rendered first at half resolution and blurred through its
  mips, seen through the films (shifted by their slope) and bent more through the liquid; photographic exposure (×2.4,
  ACES). Removed: the liquid's scattered-light term, the window-frame halo, sensor grain. 30 s at 2940 × 1412: cost median
  4.7 ms, p95 11.6 ms; flicker 0.49; blinks 34.
