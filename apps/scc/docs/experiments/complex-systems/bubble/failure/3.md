# `/bubble/3` — the foam is its own network (2026-10-07; second and third versions 2026-10-08)

- **Question:** with no external graph, can a 2D soap foam show a network by being one? Nodes are bubbles, links are the walls they share, and the dynamics are foam physics: von Neumann–Mullins coarsening (more than six walls grow, fewer shrink — a topological rich-get-richer), T1 neighbour swaps, T2 vanishing.
- **Baseline and quality bar:** [`/bubble/1`](../1.md) (= [7-glsl-6](../../adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-6.md)): its film material, its `옵션`/`닫기` grammar, 60 Hz pacing, DPR ≤ 2. See the family's [second-round lessons](../README.md#quality-bar-and-second-round-lessons-user-2026-10-08).
- **Kept from the first version:** power-diagram cells computed exactly every step, pressure from the topological charge 6 − n on the contact network, gas flux through walls (von Neumann–Mullins inside), T1 as a continuous flip, T2 at 1.5 px², nucleation at Plateau borders, and the coupled Newton step for weights.

## First version (2026-10-07) — rejected: "how is this a bubble? it's rock"

- **What it was:** the screen closed into a torus and tiled edge to edge by about 1,040 cells. Each cell was shaded as an opaque dome (exponential rim relief), filled grey, with noise-folded interference and a softbox.
- **What it got right (Node):** von Neumann slope 0.986, P(6) 31%, μ₂ 1.66, Aboav `a` 1.24, T2 85% three-sided, centroid motion p99.9 0.50 px.
- **What the lead measured in the browser** (1470×706, DPR 2): a full-screen wallpaper of dark bevelled pebbles. Flicker was 8.55/255 per 0.1 s with 739 blinking pixels per sample, against 3.66 and 638 for bubble/1.
- **Why it failed** (the family's lessons, confirmed here):
  - Wallpaper erased free faces and scale hierarchy.
  - Domes, bevels, grey fill and noise read as stone.
  - The network was a uniform texture, not relations.
  - The flicker came from sub-pixel highlights and lines on a moving tiling.
- The torus model is kept only in this record (the code was replaced in place).

## Second version (2026-10-08)

### Model (`model.ts`, `measure.ts`; `model.test.ts`)

- **A raft with free faces.** Bubble i has weight wᵢ = Rᵢ² and owns the points where its power `|p − xᵢ|² − wᵢ` is negative and least. This is the power diagram restricted to the union of the discs.
  - A free face is an arc of the bubble's own circle.
  - A wall is the radical line through the two crossing points of the circles, so walls end exactly at the free faces.
  - Where three discs fail to cover a corner, a gap (liquid) remains.
  - Each cell is clipped from a square around its disc, by the bisectors of overlapping discs and then by the disc. Free arcs are split at ≤ 60°. Area and centroid include the circular segments.
- **Pressure:** the Laplace value 1/R on free faces, and the charge-sourced Poisson equation inside: `Σⱼ lᵢⱼ(pᵢ − pⱼ) + Lᶠᵢ(pᵢ − 1/Rᵢ) = (π/3)(6 − nᵢ)` for interior cells. Free faces are sealed; gas moves only through walls.
- **Cohesion and site motion:** a gentle drift (20 px/s at the raft's nominal edge) toward the screen's middle holds the raft together, like capillarity in a bubble raft. The weight Newton step adds the free faces' `Lᶠ/2R` term. Lloyd site motion is compensated so each cell stays in place.
- **Hierarchy from coarsening:** nucleated bubbles are small (0.15–0.8 × 1,100 px², log-uniform), so they feed their neighbours and vanish while hubs grow. The page starts from 7 hubs (80% of the raft, sizes log-uniform over ×8) among 110 small bubbles, warmed for 400 steps of 1/20 s (≈ 0.25 s of CPU).
- **Parameters:** raft 30% of the screen; κ = 0.05 × 1,100 px²/s; nucleation 25 (per 1,000 reference areas per second); φ = 0.002. Slider ranges: κ 0–0.25, nucleation 0–200, φ 0–0.2. The coarsening guard is now 12–24 bubbles.

### Rendering (`foam.ts`)

- **Geometry and pass:** one instanced wedge per edge (6 triangles each), tiled from the model's own walls and free arcs. One pass at device resolution; nothing is drawn outside the raft.
- **Material:** bubble/1's `FILM_FRAGMENT`, read closely and ported:
  - a cap over the bubble's own disc (same depth, slope limited per pixel);
  - Fresnel 0.05 + 0.95(1 − cos)⁵ and an edge-on term;
  - drainage from 20 nm to 560 nm (thinner with age), tempered to 30% colour above 150 nm;
  - one window highlight (power 60) and a faint mirror (power 40).
- **Changes from bubble/1's material, each one measured:**
  - The cap is shaded from the disc, not from the distance to walls. Shading from walls showed a hub's many short, straight walls as facets. The softmax variants tried along the way either left the facets or drew a ring inside each hub.
  - Pixel position is taken from `gl_FragCoord`.
  - The window highlight scales with bubble size (`smoothstep(6, 40, R)`): on small, moving bubbles it was the main source of blinking.
  - Walls between two bubbles fade to a shared grey (0.21), so raster edges don't show.
  - Long walls (≥ 14–50 px) get a soft edge-on glow, so pressed hubs read as sharing a wall.
  - The colour fades to black over 1.5 px at free faces.
- **Network legibility:** each wall shows the model's real flux as a portion of gas. A portion grows from nothing inside the giver, crosses the wall and shrinks into the receiver. Its period is 2.6–5 s per pair, and its area is `|flux| × period × 0.3`. Portions are placed on the CPU so both cells draw the same bubble. The reading is small, few-walled bubbles feeding the hubs.
- **Budget:** about 120 bubbles and ≈ 900 edge instances × 18 vertices. Per frame it uploads cell (3 texels) and edge (2 texels) float textures. There are 2 texture lookups per pixel and no offscreen targets. Arcs that cannot affect a pixel are skipped by an exact lower bound.

## Third version (2026-10-08, after the lead's review beside bubble/1)

**The review of the second version:**
- Hubs read as grey balls on a bed of small bubbles. They rarely shared walls, and each was shaded as a sphere from its own disc.
- Interiors were hazy mid-grey.
- Transfers were 1–5 px "⊖" beads.
- Structure read only as big vs. small.
- The raft was one static cloud.

**Model changes:**
- **No more interior nucleation.** Gas is blown in as *clusters at the rim*:
  - The host is, from a random direction, the free-faced bubble farthest out.
  - A cluster is 3–7 bubbles: a lead of 0.6–3 × 1,100 px² plus small ones of 0.15–0.8 ×.
  - Each starts as a 2 px disc touching the host's free face and inflates over 3.2 s.
  - Cohesion (30 px/s) carries the clusters in, where the bubbles they touch drain them.
- **Hubs press together.** With nothing nucleated between them, they now share walls directly: 5–9 hub–hub walls, against 1–3 before.
- **Hub absorbs a neighbour.** Every 12–38 s, when there are more than three hubs (R > 60 px), the wall between two of them with the steepest pressure drop × length is chosen. The smaller drains into the larger over 7 s, and the drain is added to that wall's flux, so it is drawn there.
- **Parameters:** nucleation 8 (range 0–40); others unchanged.

**Rendering changes:**
- **Lighting:**
  - No cap, no sphere shading.
  - Interior is the film × 0.065.
  - Sparse drainage bands: 20–400 nm, colour only above 220 nm.
  - A thin rim of light (width 0.022·R, 1–2.6 px, plus half a device pixel) along every wall and free face.
  - The edge-on wall line is a brighter shared grey (0.36).
  - One window crescent (width 0.035·R, 1.4–5 px) where the rim faces up-left; the side is taken from the disc, so there are no facets. A faint mirror on the lower right.
- **Transfers are portions:**
  - Only for the ~24 strongest flows, through a threshold eased each frame toward the 24th strongest flow; portions near it fade in.
  - Radius 4.5–11 px from `|flux| × period × 0.7`.
  - Period 4.5–8 s per pair.
  - Each travels from 0.3 R deep in the giver to the same depth in the receiver, as an even film (no bands).
- The long-wall glow was removed: the shared walls now carry the light themselves.

**Node (1470 × 706, 180 s):**
- Count 61–75; largest/median radius 209–220 / 11–16 px.
- Largest four: R 209 with 24 walls, 142/13, 98/9, 44/5.
- 81 rim clusters and 3 hubs absorbed in 180 s.
- von Neumann slope 0.997 (58 interior bubbles in the 4000 × 3000 raft).
- The three largest take in 1,299 px²/s and give 280.
- T2 2.3/s, all with ≤ 4 walls.
- Centroid motion p99.9 0.56 px; max 4.1 px, at a cluster's birth.

**Browser (own tab, `localhost:2000`, canvas 2940 × 1412, under shared load):**
- Flicker 0.40–0.43 per 0.1 s; blinking 31.5–39.4 per sample.
- GPU draw median 3.8 ms, p95 6.8 ms.
- Shim frame cost median 9.6 ms, p95 17–29 ms.
- Mean luminance 6.6–6.9 (bubble/1: 7.0–7.3).
- No console errors.
- **On screen:** large bubbles press into each other with thin bright walls and visible three-way junctions; near-black interiors with faint bands; crowds of small bubbles at the rim; round portions of 9–20 px crossing walls into hubs; over minutes one hub can come to dominate after absorbing a neighbour.
