# `/bubble/4` — a planar network packed as touching bubbles (2026-10-07; round 2 2026-10-08)

- **Question:** if the foam's walls *are* the links (a wall for every edge and for nothing else), and bubble size comes from topology instead of a chosen mapping, does the network read as foam? Do films that remember being stirred, and walls thinned by traffic, make flow visible without lines?
- **Baseline:** [`/bubble/1`](../1.md) (= [7-glsl-6](../../adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-6.md)): ownership by `f_i(p) = |p − c_i|²/r_i − r_i`, depth-peeled at .75 CSS resolution, thin-film composite. Weaknesses found by reading it: walls come from discs that merely overlap, so the most visible structure carries no data; the film is stateless; `edgeOn = exp(−toEdge/1.4)` is a ~1.4 px bright line that flickered (20.3/255 per .1 s measured there); a round raft in the middle of a black screen.
- **Invariants kept from the family:** the bottom `옵션`/`닫기` grammar and CSS of bubble/1; WebGL2; frames paced to ≤ 60 Hz on whole vsyncs; DPR ≤ 2; achromatic film on dark.

## Round 1 (2026-10-07): changed variables

1. **Network (`network.ts`).** A triangulated rectangle plus one vertex at infinity (`OMEGA`, the screen frame): a maximal planar graph whose frame vertices are those adjacent to `OMEGA`. Rotation system (CCW neighbour lists); seeded (mulberry32). Seed: a staggered lattice of the target size at the screen's aspect, with an odd number of rows (a staggered frame row made a chord), then 60 s of dynamics.
   - Growth: planar preferential attachment. A face is chosen with probability ∝ the sum of its corners' degrees (a Random Apollonian Network, bubble/2, chooses uniformly); when the frame is shorter than `0.9 · 2(√(1.9·0.866·N) + √(N/(1.9·0.866)))` a frame edge is split instead. Births `0.9/s × clamp(1 + 4(1 − N/target), 0, 4)`.
   - Flux: random walk with restart, damping .85, restart ∝ fitness; log-fitness is Ornstein–Uhlenbeck (spread 1.4, relaxation 1/25 s). Edge flux `d(PR_u/deg_u + PR_v/deg_v)`, normalised to mean 1. One power step per frame (warm).
   - Film per wall: `dh/dt = 0.055(1 − h) − 0.017 φ²`. At `h = 0` the wall bursts: the two bubbles coalesce (edge contraction, link condition plus frame checks); if that is invalid, the wall swaps (flip); otherwise it is reset to .3.
   - Swaps (T1): 0.03 proposals per edge per second, Metropolis with energy `0.15·Δ Σ(deg − 6)² − 0.5·Δflux − 4·log(r_a r_b / r_c r_d)`. The last term is the short-wall rule: a wall between two large bubbles pinched by two small ones swaps.
   - Vanishing (T2): a bubble older than 8 s whose packing radius is under 3 CSS px loses walls one swap at a time (6 steps/s over all such bubbles) and disappears at three, as in a coarsening foam.
2. **Packing (`packing.ts`).** Radii by the Collins–Stephenson iteration ([Collins & Stephenson, *A circle packing algorithm*, Comput. Geom. 25, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7721(02)00099-8)), with the [circle packing theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circle_packing_theorem) (Koebe–Andreev–Thurston) guaranteeing that contacts = edges. The angle-sum targets are 2π inside, π on the frame and π/2 at four corners, so frame centres lie on a rectangle and the screen edges cut the frame bubbles in half. The foam fills the screen. 20 warm sweeps per frame; centres are laid out face by face from the highest-degree interior vertex; the corner rectangle is fitted to the screen. Corners move one frame vertex per 0.15 s toward the points that split the frame's length (Σ rᵢ + rᵢ₊₁) as W:H:W:H. The residual aspect error (corner steps are discrete) becomes a smoothed anisotropic stretch (u-space = CSS px × (σ, 1/σ)).
3. **Mapping packing → walls: none needed.** With `f_i(p) = (|p − c_i|² − r_i²)/r_i` and the packing's own radii, both f vanish at a tangency point, so every wall passes through its contact. Wherever `f_a = f_b`, `|∇f_a − ∇f_b|² = 8 − 4(r_a² + r_b² − d²)/(r_a r_b)`. This is 16 for every tangent pair (`d = r_a + r_b`), so the three wall normals at a junction have equal length and walls meet at exactly 120°. LittleBadger's contact distance `d² = r₁² + r₂² − r₁r₂` ([LittleBadger/bubbles](https://github.com/LittleBadger/bubbles)) is the same identity with constant 4, where the free circles intersect. A least-squares conversion of the packing to that distance is overdetermined (≈3 equations per bubble); the first attempt diverged and was dropped.
4. **Drawn motion (`scene.ts`).** Bubbles follow their packing circles with exact critically damped springs (2.6 rad/s). Newborns grow from 0 in their junction. A burst bubble stays as a ghost of the bubble it merged with (same group: no wall between them) while it shrinks; a vanished one keeps its last walls. A swapped wall lingers in the lists for 2.5 s, and second neighbours whose drawn discs touch get a wall, so geometry never shows a seam while it moves.
5. **Rendering (`foam.ts`), 5 draw calls per frame:**
   - Ownership: one instanced splat (discs `1.35r + 4`) at .5 CSS resolution in u-space, depth = f, R32F + DEPTH24. Replaces two depth-peeled layers.
   - Stream (.5 res, RGBA16F): a stream function inside each cell, windowed to 0 at its walls (smooth-min distance, k = 4 px), so the film moves along walls and never across them. It holds swirl `5.5r·(noise − .75)` and a Marangoni-like drift `7r·U(n × q)` toward walls with flux above .9, plus the equilibrium thickness: draining to the top, fed by borders, thinned to black film beside busy walls.
   - Film (.5 res, RGBA16F ping-pong, 2 substeps): semi-Lagrangian advection by curl ψ plus the bubble's drawn velocity (as in [stable fluids](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/public_user/stam/reality/Research/pdf/ns.pdf), without a pressure solve because a curl is divergence-free). It then relaxes to equilibrium with τ = 7 s (memory), or τ = 0 (memory off: stateless, as bubble/1).
   - Composite (device px): owner from 4 texels, corrected exactly through the wall list. Each wall is a liquid band of half-width `mix(.7, 3.4, liquid)·(.38 + .62 h)`, joined at junctions by an [exponential smooth-min](https://iquilezles.org/articles/smin/) with `k = mix(.9, 7.5, liquid²)` into Plateau borders. The cell is a shallow cap with [two-beam interference](https://www.alanzucconi.com/2017/10/27/carpaint-shader-thin-film-interference/) of the remembered thickness (12–1062 nm), 13% chroma at most, plus Fresnel and a window highlight.
   - Flicker cause addressed: the bright line is replaced by liquid at least ~1 CSS px (2 device px) wide, area-filtered by the pixel footprint (`clamp(.5 − edge/footprint)`), so brightness integrates continuously as walls move.
6. **Options:** `막의 기억` (memory, on), `흐름` (traffic thinning and drift, on; rendering only, the network still drains walls), liquid `마른–젖은` (.28), `거품` target (120–640, 300). Tap the middle of a bubble (< .55 r) to focus its walls (wider, brighter); tap elsewhere to insert a bubble into the nearest junction. Keys N and Escape. A screen-reader summary every 2 s. Reduced motion holds the network and the film and snaps the springs.

## Round 1 measurements (Node, 1470 × 762 CSS)

- **Edge ↔ wall** (`packing.test.ts`; seed 11, 3 min of dynamics then settled):

  - 1 px raster (scratch): 801 adjacent pairs, 0 non-edges, 775/775 on-screen contacts seen as walls, 289/289 bubbles own pixels;
  - 2 px raster (test): 688 pairs, all links, 629/629 contacts seen;
  - at rest the renderer's wall lists equal the graph neighbours exactly.
- **Sizes:** radius min 2.0, median 25.6, max 253 px (250 bubbles); no bubble older than its grace period is under 3 px.
- **Failures on the way:** degree-balance flips alone with a one-hub seed gave radii from 0 to 500 px (median 1 px; 200 of 300 under 3 px). Fixed corners squeezed whole sides exponentially; arc-length corners did not cure it. Apollonian nesting from preferential insertion persisted until the short-wall swap and T2 vanishing were added.
- **Dynamics:** 10 min, topology only: size 263, 713 births, 380 bursts, 388 vanishings, 5,250 swaps, max degree 12, mean 5.51. Scene with packing, 3 min: 255 bubbles, largest 14 walls; per minute 70 births, 41 bursts, 40 vanishings, 504 swaps.
- **Cost:**
  - CPU `stepScene + fillRenderer` per frame: p50 0.78 ms, p95 1.93 ms, p99 2.66 ms; one 26 ms outlier in 3 min, not reproduced. Scene creation ~100 ms.
  - Cold Collins–Stephenson solve ~2,800 sweeps (51 ms); warm ~23 sweeps to 1e-5 after an event.
  - Stretch `|ln σ|`: p50 .024, p90 .045, max .055.
- **GPU budget (computed, unmeasured):**
  - field textures at 735 × 381: R32F owners + depth + RGBA16F stream + 2 × RGBA16F film, ≈ 7 MB;
  - data textures 128 × 16 and 1536 × 16 RGBA32F;
  - composite 2940 × 1524 = 4.48 M px, each about 4 owner + 4 bubble fetches, ~3 fetches per wall (mean ~5.5 walls, ≤ 24 slots) and 1 film fetch;
  - no particles; ≤ 1,024 instanced quads.

## Round 2 (2026-10-08): changed variables

1. **Free raft instead of wallpaper (`packing.ts`).**
   - The frame no longer has hard corners. It turns through 2π shared among its vertices, by vertex count along the frame:
     - a base weight `0.16 + 0.1·wobble(time)` on every vertex;
     - Gaussian bumps of 2.2 (width .05) at four soft corners;
     - at most π/4 per vertex.
   - Positions by arc length failed: a vertex given more turn grows, takes more length and drags its corner, so the raft stayed round (aspect 1.07).
   - Weighting turn by a vertex's span ran away: one frame bubble reached 266 px.
   - The aspect follows the screen through `share`, the bottom side's share of the frame. A first sign error pinned it at its limit.
   - The raft is oriented by the principal axis of the frame centres, kept continuous between frames.
   - It is fitted inside the screen with a 3.5% black margin, free faces included (`f_i = 0.45 r_i + 2`, free radius ≈ 1.2 r).
   - Node: the raft uses 89–93% × 92% of the screen; nothing is cut by an edge.
2. **Hubs (`network.ts`).**
   - Faces are chosen ∝ (corner-degree sum)^2.5; degree balancing is off; the degree cap is 30.
   - The short-wall swap now uses `log(min(r_a, r_b)/min(r_c, r_d))`. The old `log(r_a r_b/r_c r_d)` removed every hub's walls to its crowd. An intermediate `max` version left newborns stuck under 1 px.
   - Hubs now reach degree 17–23 and about 5–9× the median radius (Node, 4 min).
   - Trade-off: a larger hub squeezes its crowd under 3 px, and T2 then removes them. Visibility, not the degree cap, limits hub size.
3. **Directed transfers (`scene.ts`).**
   - Net flow along a link is `d(PR_u/deg_u − PR_v/deg_v)` over the mean link flux. Links above .6 carry portions, at most 24.
   - A portion is a small bubble (≤ 8 px, ≤ .28 of the smaller bubble) that buds at the contact, crosses the wall and is taken in, each link at its own period (2.6–5.2 s).
   - Portions are part of the foam (wall lists {source, target}). They start at radius 0 and fade over .25 s if their link goes, so nothing pops.
   - In the browser they radiate from hubs into their crowds.
   - At .3 / 96 portions (first try), the blinking pixels doubled.
4. **Performance (`foam.ts`, `scene.ts`).**
   - Half-resolution **field** pass: exact owner (one correction pass over its wall list), its two nearest walls with film and flux, and the distance to the nearest edge.
   - **Interior** pass, also half resolution: cells shaded where the 3 × 3 neighbourhood is one cell, far from its edges.
   - The device-pixel **composite** takes those as is. Elsewhere it evaluates only the walls the owner's texel names plus the other texel owners (≤ 6, constant-index arrays).
   - The film runs at ⅓ CSS resolution, one step per frame.
   - The wall-slot texture (34 per bubble) is uploaded only when the lists change. Before, about 590 KB went up every frame.
   - Packing solve and layout run on even frames, the network on odd ones (adaptive sweeps, tolerance 2e-5). Reduced motion also holds the frame's wobble.
   - Rejected on the way:
     - dynamically indexed 12-candidate arrays (slow);
     - a stencil split (added a full-screen pass);
     - a texel-skirt outside the raft (texels beyond the free face kept an owner; now marked outside).
5. **Look (bubble/1's `FILM_FRAGMENT` as reference):** near-black transparent interiors (face-on film × .1005), light at the rim (Fresnel over a band of .08 r + 1.5 px), liquid walls, one soft highlight per bubble (none under 6 px radius), faint drainage bands. Liquid fills junction gaps but ends 1.5 wall-widths past the free face.
