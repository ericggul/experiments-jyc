# `/bubble/5` — a foam computed site by site on the GPU (2026-10-07, browser observation pending)

- **Question:** can the foam's form *grow* from GPU state (as in [7-glsl-3](../../adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-3.md) and [7-glsl-5](../../adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-5.md)) rather than be placed by the CPU and shaded? The network is the foam's own contact graph: bubbles sharing wall sites. It runs a network process: gas moves across every shared wall from higher to lower pressure.
- **Baseline:** [`/bubble/1`](../1.md) for the UI grammar (CSS module copied; `옵션`/`닫기` toggle closed by default; one-line Korean hint; sliders) and the thin-film composite (tempered grey interference at 650/532/450 nm, Fresnel weight, window highlight and its faint mirror). Everything that makes the form is new; [7-glsl-6](../../adaptive-coevolving-network/7-glsl-6.md)'s CPU-placed circles, depth peeling and overlap walls are not used.
- **Code:** `components/complex-systems/bubble/5/`. `potts.ts` holds the parameters, ids, first foam and a CPU replica. `foam.ts` holds the WebGL2 passes and shaders. `passes.ts` is the GPU frame pass by pass in TypeScript. `potts.test.ts` holds the node tests, `measure.ts` the measurements, and `index.tsx` the route.

## Changed variable: the foam is an extended Potts model on the GPU

- **Model:** a cellular Potts model ([Graner & Glazier, PRL 69, 2013 (1992)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.69.2013); [Glazier & Graner, PRE 47, 2128 (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.47.2128)) on a lattice at 0.5 × CSS resolution (735 × 381 = 280,035 sites at 1470 × 762). It minimises `H = J·Σ[σᵢ≠σⱼ] + Σ_b (λ/T_b)(A_b − T_b)²`.
  - Wall pairs within distance √5 (20 neighbours) keep walls round rather than lattice-aligned ([Holm, Glazier, Srolovitz & Grest, PRA 43, 2662 (1991)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.43.2662)).
  - J = 1, λ = 12, temperature 2 (slider 1.2–4).
  - Copy attempts go to one of 8 neighbours.
  - The screen edge is a free boundary, so walls meet it square and the foam reads as continuing past the screen.
- **Parallel updates:** nine 3 × 3 sublattice passes per sweep, in a shuffled order. Two sites of a class are ≥ 3 apart, beyond each other's reach, so each pass is exact. This is the checkerboard idea of [Tapia & D'Souza, Comput. Phys. Commun. 182, 857 (2011)](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Parallelizing+the+cellular+Potts+model+on+graphics+processing+units). There are 2 sweeps (Monte Carlo steps) per frame, and areas are counted once per frame.
- **Per-bubble reductions without atomics:** one point per site, sent with `texelFetch(gl_VertexID)` to its bubble's texel (128 × 32 RGBA32F, MRT, `EXT_float_blend` additive). Each point adds the bubble's area, centroid offset, second moments, wall pairs and gas loss. A second scatter of 4,096 points into one texel gives the total gas.
- **Network process (gas exchange):** pressure is `p = 2λ(1 − A/T)`, and the target T is the bubble's gas.
  - Across every 4-neighbour wall pair, gas `D·dt·(p_b − p_c)/(1 + D·dt·(L_b k_b + L_c k_c))` moves from b to c, where L = wall pairs and k = ∂p/∂T.
  - This is conservative (the same amount leaves one bubble as enters the other) and stable for any D. Diffusion D = 0.5 (slider 0–3).
  - Gas is renormalised each frame by the last total so that targets sum to the lattice, and the foam always fills the screen.
  - Small bubbles, whose walls curve most, empty into large ones: Ostwald ripening, the physical rich-get-richer. In a dry 2D foam this is von Neumann's law, `dA/dt ∝ n − 6` ([Glazier, Gross & Stavans, PRA 36, 306 (1987)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.36.306)).
  - Below 3 sites of gas a bubble gives the rest up and dissolves (T2).
- **Steady state:** each bubble divides when its gas and its area both exceed its own split size.
  - Split size is 3,000 CSS px² × a factor of 0.7–2.4 hashed from its id and birth, so mature bubbles differ widely.
  - The division is a chord across the long axis (from the second moments). The child takes 15–50 % of the bubble.
- **Ids are claimed without any list or read-back:** every frame, slots are paired by a random rotation of the slot ring. A bubble may take its partner slot only if that slot is free. The free slot evaluates the same function of the same texels and agrees.
- **Taps and nucleation:**
  - A tap blows a bubble of radius 9 sites (18 CSS px) through the same pairing. The request is offered for 10 frames; a serial stops it from re-blowing.
  - Nucleation is a Poisson process of radius-3 nuclei: 0.8 per million CSS px² per second (slider 0–4).
- **Display (no staircase, calm):**
  - **Labels:** per site, the two strongest ids in a Gaussian window (σ 0.95–1.95 sites with wetness, reach √10), eased with τ = 0.3 s.
  - **Wall distance:** chamfer distance relaxed in place every frame (16 neighbours incl. knight moves), eased with τ = 0.35 s.
  - **Device-resolution reconstruction:** labels and distance are read through a quadratic B-spline with analytic derivatives. Walls are the zero set of `χ_a − χ_b`, a soft line at least 1.5 device px wide.
  - **Plateau borders:** liquid where the leading share `χ_a/Σχ` falls below 0.42–0.58 (the wetness slider). That is junctions when dry, and every wall when wet. The edge is anti-aliased by the share's analytic gradient over the pixel footprint; the borders have a dark core, a bright meniscus and a glint.
  - **Film memory (RGBA16F):** thickness is stirred by the curl of a slow noise stream function per bubble, plus a slight spin. It drains toward black film (τ 11 s), is renewed in thick patches at the walls (marginal regeneration), and is thick in newborn bubbles.
  - **Cap:** a shallow cap from the wall distance gives Fresnel, highlight and depth.
- **Interaction:**
  - **Tap:** blows a bubble; Enter/Space blows one in the middle.
  - **Sliders:** 잔잔–들썩 (temperature), 느리게–빠르게 (gas diffusion), 새 거품 없음–많이 (nucleation), 마른–젖은 (wetness).
  - **Screen reader:** the summary comes from a 16-byte asynchronous PBO read of the totals texel (fence, never blocking).
  - **Reduced motion:** stops the simulation and film; a tap runs them for 2.5 s.
  - **Resize:** a resize that changes the lattice size starts a new foam.

## Measured in Node (`node measure.ts …`, `node --test potts.test.ts`)

- **Tests (9 pass):**
  - the local ΔH equals the change of the full Hamiltonian, edges included (400 random flips);
  - no offset reaches across a sublattice class;
  - slot pairing is a bijection with no self-pairs;
  - chord offsets cut the intended share (±0.5 %);
  - the gas exchange is conservative to 1e-9;
  - the replica foam averages 6 ± 0.4 sides with gas equal to the lattice;
  - the emulated GPU frame never leaves a site with a dead id, blows a tap exactly once at about πr² sites, keeps its count, and keeps gas within 2 %;
  - labels ease by the blend.
- **Shaders:** all ten GLSL ES 3.00 sources pass Khronos `glslang` 16.6.0 (run outside the repository).
- **Steady run (GPU frame emulated, 1470 × 762 CSS px, defaults, last 40 s of 60 s):**
  - 496 bubbles (447–547), still coarsening toward steady state from the denser first foam (births 2.8/s, vanishings 5.3/s).
  - Inner bubbles have 5.97 sides (5.95 counting only contacts ≥ 3 pairs).
  - Area 10/50/90 % is 692 / 2,240 / 4,788 CSS px², largest 6,552.
  - 3.0 % of sites change per frame; total gas / lattice = 1.0000.
  - Earlier scans at λ 24 settled near 360–440 bubbles.
- **Coarsening without division (CPU replica):** ⟨A⟩ − A(5 s) ∝ t^0.97 for t ≥ 30 s, the linear growth of 2D foam (von Neumann). The raw log-log slope of ⟨A⟩ is 0.61.
- **Wall motion per frame (persisting crossings, CSS px):** raw lattice median 0.38, 99 % 1.44. Displayed labels median 0.056, 99 % 0.40.
- **Found and fixed on the way:**
  1. Gas exchange explicit and unconditionally divided per bubble was not conservative. Total gas drained, the renormalisation ran away, and births/vanishings reached 33/s. Fixed by the symmetric per-pair form.
  2. At λ = 24, areas counted once per frame made small bubbles overshoot with a two-frame period (a blown bubble: 154 → 125 → 172 → … → 79 sites in 11 frames) while its noisy pressure pumped its gas out. λ = 12 is stable.
  3. Rigid spin carried renewed film only along the walls, so interiors drained to black and read as rings, a rejected 7-glsl-6 outcome. The stream-function swirl carries it inward.
  4. Without a per-bubble split size the area spread was only 1:3.

## Budget (above the [GPU safety](../../../../../../../docs/harness/webgpu-tsl-particles.md) default; needs the observation below)

- **Rate:** ≤ 60 Hz on whole vsyncs (`FRAME_INTERVAL = 1000/60 − 3`), steps of at most 1/30 s. DPR is capped at 2.
- **Lattice:** 0.5 × CSS (280,035 sites at 1470 × 762).
- **Point primitives per frame:** 280,035 scatter points plus 4,096 totals points.
- **Draw calls per frame:**
  - 1 scatter, 1 state, 1 totals, 1 division, 18 sweep passes, 1 labels, 1 surface, 1 film: 25 in all.
  - Plus 3 buffer clears, and a 16-byte PBO read every 2 s.
- **Fragment work per frame:**
  - Sweeps: 18 × 280k fragments, with 1/9 active (~24 fetches each).
  - Labels: 280k × ~37 fetches.
  - Surface: 280k × ~25 fetches + 5 value noises.
  - Film: 2940 × 1524 = 4.48 M fragments × 18 fetches.
- **Texture memory:**
  - lattice R32F × 2: 2.2 MB
  - labels RGBA32F × 2: 9.0 MB
  - surface RGBA16F × 2: 2.2 MB
  - per-bubble state, sums and totals: 0.5 MB
  - Total ≈ 14 MB, plus the 18 MB drawing buffer.
- **Requirements:** `EXT_color_buffer_float` and `EXT_float_blend`; without them the route warns and draws nothing.
- **CPU per frame:** uniforms and the class order only; no per-frame uploads.
- **GPU frame time: not measured.** The target is ≤ 8 ms on an M-series MacBook at DPR 2.
