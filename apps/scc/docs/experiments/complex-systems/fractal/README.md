# Fractal experiments

Self-similar structures where every part holds smaller copies of the whole.
Routes live at `/fractal/<family>/<n>`; the index is `/fractal`.

- [clock/1–3](clock/README.md): analogue clocks held at the tips of clock
  hands. Moved here from `/clock/1–3` on 2026-10-05; the old routes redirect.
- [logo/1](logo/1.md): an AI or tech logo (official Gemini sparkle by default)
  holding smaller copies of itself on each of its symmetry tips, or a Mix of all.
