# fractal/logo/1

Route: `/fractal/logo/1`. Date: 2026-10-05.

## Premise

A logo whose shape is unchanged by every quarter-turn and mirror can be its own
fractal: put a copy of half the size on each tip, then repeat. The field is
still; the participant swaps logos and parameters to compare the results.

## Which logo

Symmetry was measured, not recalled. 75 AI-service marks from
[@lobehub/icons-static-svg](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg)
were rasterised at 256 px, cropped to their bounding box and compared with
their 90° rotation and both mirrors (intersection over union):

| mark | 90° | mirror x | mirror y |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Gemma | 0.947 | 0.948 | 0.947 |
| Sora | 0.678 | 0.738 | 0.725 |
| OpenAI | 0.215 | 0.326 | 0.326 |
| Claude | 0.400 | 0.384 | 0.487 |

Only Gemini has the full eight-fold (D4) symmetry. Gemma is close but not
exact.

The table above tested only 90° and the mirrors. A later check of 60° and 120°
turns (n-fold rotation about the alpha centroid, 1024 px) found more:

| mark | 120° | 60° | tips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | 0.979 | 0.980 | 6, first at 13.25°, radius 12.15–12.17 of 24 |
| Qwen | 0.934 | 0.309 | 3, first at 72.75°, radius ≈11.4 (approximate) |

Copilot keeps only 180° (0.984). Every other AI mark has no rotational
symmetry and uses its box's edge midpoints.

Other tech marks: all 3,447 icons in `simple-icons` 16.25.0 were measured.
Microsoft and Windows are not in that package. Kept were the marks at or above
0.995 on 90° and both mirrors, measured again at 1024 px:

| mark | source | tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft | [Wikimedia Commons `Microsoft_icon.svg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_icon.svg) | corners, 45° |
| Square | simple-icons | corners, 45° (rounded) |
| CodeSandbox | simple-icons | corners, 45° |
| Material Design | simple-icons | corners, 45° |
| Hashnode | simple-icons | axes, 0° |
| Okta | simple-icons | axes, 0° (a circle) |

Loom (0.995) was left out. Its burst is twelve-fold, so four tips would
misrepresent it. Target (0.981), Kentico and Headspace fall below the threshold.

## Assets

All files sit unmodified in `public/images/fractal-logo/`.

- **Gemini (default):** `gemini_sparkle_aurora_33f86dc0c0257da337c63.svg`, the
  file served by [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) on 2026-10-05
  (`https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_aurora_33f86dc0c0257da337c63.svg`,
  SHA-1 `44b6416d2c0b47875bc345b4adc093655f701033`). It is a clip path
  (viewBox 192, tips at 8 and 184) over an embedded 472×461 JPEG aurora. Rendered
  at 512 px, its shape matches Google's 512 px PNG favicon with 0 differing
  alpha pixels.
- **Tech marks:** Microsoft is the Wikimedia Commons file (10-unit squares,
  1-unit gap, as published there). It is not served by microsoft.com, which was
  not reachable for comparison. The rest come from simple-icons 16.25.0 and are
  filled with each mark's registered brand hex.
- **Other AI marks:** Gemma, ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, Meta AI, Mistral,
  Grok, DeepSeek and Qwen from
  [@lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.1](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg).
  These are community redraws, not files served by each company. ChatGPT and
  Grok use `currentColor`; the renderer substitutes black, their official mark
  colour.

The renderer fetches each SVG and changes only two things: it gives the SVG an
intrinsic size of 2048 px, and it substitutes `currentColor`. It then
rasterises sprites from 8 to 2048 px from that vector. No copy is upscaled from a
smaller raster, recoloured or rotated.

## Model

- Every copy is `scale` times its parent and is centred on a tip. A logo with
  measured n-fold rotation has n tips at its rotation centre's outermost points:
  ChatGPT 6, Qwen 3, and 4 for Gemini and the tech marks. Every other logo uses
  the edge midpoints of its content box, measured from a 4096 px rasterisation.
- The root holds a copy on every tip. Every other copy skips the tip pointing
  straight back into its parent. Odd orders (Qwen) have no such tip and keep all
  three. `back tip on` keeps the back tip as well.
- **Mix** keeps the four edge-midpoint tips of a square frame. Every logo is
  fitted to that square by its content box. The root is Gemini. A child on tip
  k takes logo `(parent + 1 + k) mod 17`, so it differs from its parent and
  from its siblings.
- At scale 0.5 the six-tip ChatGPT fractal overlaps itself densely: each copy
  holds five copies. Lower scales open it.
- The fractal is static and centred. Along every branch, offsets add up per axis
  to at most `max(body, maxTip / (1 − scale))`, so the root is sized to fill 92% of the
  viewport. Levels are laid out until copies fall below 0.75 px, `depth` is
  reached, or the 8,192-sprite budget would be exceeded. When a level would go
  over the budget, all of it is dropped.
- `parent over` (default) draws deepest first, so each parent's tips stay
  whole. `child over` cuts the parent tips; it is kept for comparison.

## Controls

A collapsed `expand` button sits at the lower right. It opens the logo choice
(AI marks, tech marks, Mix), `scale` (0.30–0.60, default 0.50), `depth` (0–11,
or ∞ by default), `back tip` and `parent/child over`. Each change redraws once. Nothing animates.

## History

On 2026-10-05 the first version eased into an endless automatic zoom. The user
had not asked for it and rejected it, so it was removed the same day. Do not
add camera motion unless it is requested.

## Status

Pure model tests pass. The route has not been observed in a browser.
Sharpness at DPR 1 on retina displays and SVG decoding of each logo are
unverified.
