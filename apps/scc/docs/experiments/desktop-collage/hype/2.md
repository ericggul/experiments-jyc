# hype/2 — all at once, then flicking

Route: `/desktop-collage/hype/2`, created 2026-10-10. Control endpoint:
`/desktop-collage/hype/2/control`; cloned pages at
`/desktop-collage/hype/2/window`. Implementation:
`components/desktop-collage/hype/2/`, a complete copy of
[hype/1](README.md) with its rhythm, reader and material replaced; nothing is
imported across the two.

## Tested relation

Every window opens at the start, in quick succession, and stays; together
they fill the screen. From the moment a few have loaded, the front window
changes many times a second for twenty seconds, at random among them, in
flurries and never pausing, with a small scroll now and then, a new place
and size for one flick in three, and every few seconds a reshuffle of
several windows at once. Nobody reads: the
attention is the flicking itself. The material is the fear of missing out
around AI: the jobs it will take, the stocks that will moon, the graduates
nobody hires, the courses everyone else has finished, the forecast that
became a deadline. Some windows are real pages, some are cloned services,
and some are just a picture or a video opened on its own.

Question: when the flood stops arriving and starts flickering, does the
desktop read as one anxious attention rather than as many pages? Baseline:
hype/1, whose windows arrive over minutes and are read.

## How it works

- **Burst** (`model/rhythm.ts`): `gap` (0–1500 ms, default 120, varied
  0.4–1.8×) opens the windows one after another, 24 in about three
  seconds; 0 launches them all at the same instant, which was measured
  below to place them within a tenth of a second but was felt to stall
  the machine, so the quick succession is the default. The surface opens
  the first, waits until the instance accepts hand-offs (about a second
  cold), then the rest. No limit: all stay; 6–40 of them, default 24.
- **Flicking** (`plan.ts`): from the moment four windows have settled (the
  rest join as they arrive; 25 s at most), a 10 ms loop raises a random
  open window
  (`Target.activateTarget`, the browser-level command that reorders
  windows; `Page.bringToFront` only activates a tab and was the reason the
  first version did not visibly flick) for `duration` seconds (10–300,
  default 30). Gaps are log-uniform from `pace`/8 to `pace` ms (pace 40–600,
  default 120, so 15–120 ms with most under 40) and 15 % of flicks start a
  flurry of three to six flicks 12–30 ms apart; there are no pauses. 35 %
  of flicks scroll the window a little, `moves` % (0–100, default 30) move
  and resize it to a fresh place from the same layout, and every 2–4 s a
  reshuffle gives three to six windows new places and sizes together. The
  loop ends by itself and tells the control (`done`), so the run stops
  counting as moving; the windows remain. Cost per flick is one DevTools
  command, sometimes three.
- **Windows** (`model/layout.ts`): the stack has to fill the screen. A
  fifth of the windows are the whole visible desktop; 35 % are large
  (70–100 % of its width, 70–100 % of its height) and sit against an edge
  or in a corner nine times in ten; 30 % are medium (45–75 % wide, 45–80 %
  high) and 15 % small (30–45 % wide, 30–50 % high), each coordinate
  snapping to an edge half the time. `size` (50–100 %, default 100) scales
  all of them. Pages in narrow windows are zoomed out only mildly now
  (1024 pt reference, never below 0.75); a share is forced dark.
- **Deck** (`model/catalogue.ts`): 63 real pages and 94 clones over the
  topics AI jobs, AI stocks, AI layoffs, learn AI, CS graduates, AGI 2027,
  AI bubble, NVIDIA, AI tools, prompt engineering, AI 자격증, AI 수혜주, AI 대체
  직업. Real: Google News and Naver news searches for each fear, YouTube
  searches, 18 YouTube videos found through YouTube's own search for the
  FOMO queries (titles and channels confirmed through oEmbed), Layoffs.fyi,
  Will Robots Take My Job, AI 2027, Situational Awareness, Metaculus,
  There's An AI For That, Product Hunt, Yahoo Finance and TradingView NVDA,
  Naver finance, 원티드/사람인/잡코리아/LinkedIn/Indeed job searches, Hacker
  News jobs, Coursera, Udemy, r/cscareerquestions, YC's AI companies, the
  WEF jobs report, Epoch, Our World in Data, Google Trends and two Wikipedia
  articles. Clones: Slack, Signal article, Medium listicle ("10 jobs that
  won't exist by 2027"), LinkedIn Jobs ("over 100 applicants", entry level
  0 results), a trading-app quote page (NVDA or SK하이닉스, green chart, AI
  watchlist), a Blind post (팀 절반 정리, 컴공 백수), Google results, YouTube
  watch pages around the real videos, landing pages (AI 자격증 2주 완성,
  prompt engineering bootcamp), 17 image files (Wikipedia lead images of
  Huang, Altman, Musk, Amodei, Zuckerberg, a data centre, Nvidia's campus, a
  pharmacy robot, Optimus, an unemployment chart, a crash chart, and stills
  of the videos named like screenshots) shown in Chrome's own image viewer,
  and the videos opened on their own, full-window.
- **Reader**: none. The cloned pages keep their composers but nobody writes.

Parameters: windows 6–40 (24), gap 0–1500 ms (120), for (duration, 5–300,
default 20), pace, moves, size 50–100 % (100), pages, cloned pages 0–100 %
(60), dark pages, sound, pattern, clear before a run.

## Evidence

chain's topics, containment, proportions and the width bound, deck
well-formedness (unique IDs, https for real pages, pages, images and videos
that exist, no repeats at 100 % clones, only real at 0 %), composed runs on
three displays with image and video parameters on the window route, and the
quote chart's reproducibility. They pass with hype/1's and the helper's;
`pnpm --filter @scc/archive typecheck` passes; ESLint reports no errors
(warnings: `<img>` and the Google Fonts link).

## Open questions

- Whether 15–120 ms flicking with flurries reads as panic or as a strobe;
  the stillness share may need to grow.
- Whether moved windows should animate (DevTools moves jump) and whether a
  move should ever send a window partly off-screen.
- Which fears are missing: housing, dating, the next keyword after AI.
