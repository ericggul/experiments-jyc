# hype/3 — a scatter of phones

Route: `/desktop-collage/hype/3`, created 2026-10-11. Control endpoint:
`/desktop-collage/hype/3/control`; cloned pages at
`/desktop-collage/hype/3/window`. Implementation:
`components/desktop-collage/hype/3/`, a copy of [hype/2](2.md) with its
layout replaced by scattered phones and every window rendered as a phone; nothing is
imported across the two.

## Tested relation

The same AI fear-of-missing-out material as hype/2, but every window is a
phone, the same phone every time, `height` % of the visible desktop tall
(default 80) and a phone's width for that height, dropped anywhere on the
desktop at random, so they overlap in a scatter. Each page is served and
laid out as a phone page (mobile user agent, touch, a 390 CSS px phone
viewport scaled down to the window, and the clones' own phone layouts), so
the desktop reads as a pile of phones. Two dozen pour in over a few
seconds, then one attention flicks among them for ten seconds as in hype/2,
without ever moving a window.

Question: does the same flood read differently as a pile of phones, every
screen the shape of the one in the pocket? An orderly grid (two rows of
fourteen phones, 2026-10-11 13:12) was tried first and did not give the
feeling wanted.
Baseline: hype/2's browser-sized windows.

## How it works

- **Phones** (`model/layout.ts`): height = `height` % of the desktop
  (30–100, default 80), width = height / 2.05; on this Mac 329 × 674 pt
  (70 % was tried first and raised to 80 % the same minute).
  Each window's place is uniform over the desktop (seeded), inside it. No
  sizes vary and nothing moves. Chrome accepted app windows down to 205 pt
  wide in the grid version.
- **Phone rendering** (surface `mobile`, `plan.ts` `prepare`): as soon as
  a window is placed, its page gets Safari-on-iPhone's user agent and
  Chrome's mobile emulation (touch, viewport meta honoured, device scale
  2) laid out at a phone's 390 CSS px width and scaled to fit the window
  (0.84 at 329 pt), then a reload so sites serve their phone versions
  (m.youtube.com, Google News' mobile layout, Naver's mobile pages).
  Without the scaling the first two-row run laid pages out at 205 CSS px
  and they were cramped (headlines breaking mid-word); with it each
  window shows a whole phone screen small. The page's load state is read
  only 800 ms after the reload was asked for, so the old page's
  "complete" is not taken for the new one's; 12 s after placement a
  window counts as loaded regardless.
- **Clones**: every cloned page's stylesheet has a phone layout under
  600 px (one column, menus and search boxes gone, the YouTube player full
  width, the Slack channel without rail or sidebar).
- **Sites** (`model/catalogue.ts` `sites`, `controller/sites.tsx`): the
  deck's pages grouped by service, real sites by host (Google News 9,
  Naver 10 counting its news searches and m.stock quote pages, YouTube
  25, Wikipedia 2, and one each of Layoffs.fyi, Will Robots Take My Job,
  AI 2027, Situational Awareness, Metaculus, Yahoo Finance, TradingView,
  원티드, 사람인, 잡코리아, Hacker News, Coursera, Y Combinator, WEF, Epoch,
  Our World in Data, Google Trends) and the cloned services (Slack 4,
  Signal 8, Medium 7, LinkedIn 3, quote app 3, Blind 5, Google 7, YouTube
  18, landing page 4, image files 17, video files 18). The control lists
  them as toggles with their counts; none chosen means every site, and
  any number can be chosen. Only the chosen sites' pages are drawn, the
  clone share permitting; when the chosen sites have none of the kind
  wanted (Naver has no clones) the other kind is drawn rather than another
  site.
- **Flicking**: hype/2's dwell, returns, flurries, snags and scrolls,
  without moves or rearrangements; the 10 s count from three quarters
  loaded (30 s after the start at the latest).

Parameters: windows 3–40 (24), gap 0–1500 ms (250), for (duration, 5–300,
default 10), dwell (pace, 60–1000 ms, 220), height 30–100 % (80), sites
(any set, default every site), cloned pages
0–100 % (60), dark pages 0–100 % (30), sound, pattern, clear before a run.

## Evidence

Pure tests (`model/model.test.ts`, 7): burst, dwell distribution, chain,
the phones (height share, phone proportion, same size throughout, inside
the desktop, scattered and spread across it, 329 × 674 on this Mac at 80 %), the deck, composed runs on three displays
with no zoom parameter, the sites (they cover the deck, Naver alone is
the only site drawn even at 100 % clones, two chosen sites both appear,
unknown sites are refused), and the quote chart. They pass with hype/2's and
the registry's; `pnpm typecheck` passes; ESLint reports no errors (5
warnings: `<img>` and the Google Fonts link).

2026-10-11 13:01–13:02, first version (three columns, one row of 490 ×
843 phones, 20 s), run through the real control code from a script: 24
opened, the flicking ended by itself 31.8 s after the start, and the
captures showed three phone columns with m.youtube.com, Google News
mobile, Our World in Data mobile and the clones' phone layouts. Rejected
at once: only three phones in view; the ask had been two rows of half
height and correspondingly narrow, so that many phones show.

2026-10-11 13:11–13:12, two rows (7 × 2 cells of 205 × 421 pt, 24
windows, 10 s): 24 opened, every window 205 pt wide by the window server,
the run ended by itself at 21.4 s. The capture showed fourteen phones edge
to edge, but the pages were laid out at 205 CSS px and cramped. With the
390 px phone viewport scaled to the window (next run, ended at 24.6 s,
flicking slower while the heavier reloads were in: 19 front changes at
250 ms sampling): every cell showed a whole phone screen small, the Signal
article with its thumbnail, a CNBC video, the YouTube watch clone with
comments, Google News mobile for two queries, m.youtube.com with a KNN
video, Our World in Data with its cookie banner, the LinkedIn clone's
list, Agentry, YC's startup page, `zuck-2026.jpg`, Promptcraft. One image
file (`IMG_4471.jpg`, a ytimg still) showed as a broken image in the
phone rendering, where it had loaded in hype/2; not diagnosed.

2026-10-11 13:2x, after "not the feeling I want": the grid replaced by the
scatter above. Run through the real control code from a script (24
phones of 288 × 590, 10 s): the run ended by itself at 23.6 s, 33 front
changes at 250 ms sampling. Captures showed the phones overlapping across
the whole desktop in a loose pile, each a whole phone screen (Google News
mobile, m.youtube.com, the YouTube and Signal clones, Will Robots Take My
Job, the LinkedIn clone, Promptcraft, the pictures), with the one in front
changing between captures.

2026-10-11 13:28, sites option: a run with only Naver chosen (12 phones)
put nothing but Naver in front: 엔비디아 — Npay 증권, 개발자 채용 감소, AI
수혜주, 엔비디아 주가, AI 대체 직업, SK하이닉스 HBM, AI 해고, AI 자격증
searches, all at the phone width; the capture showed the Naver news
searches and the Npay quote page in their mobile layouts overlapping
