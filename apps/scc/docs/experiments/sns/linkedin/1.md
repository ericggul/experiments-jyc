# SNS LinkedIn 1

`/sns/linkedin/1` is a local responsive LinkedIn home/jobs reconstruction; it neither connects to LinkedIn nor claims its ranking. Desktop uses a 52px global bar and measured `225/555/300px` three-column feed at 1128px. It retains profile rail, composer/sort/posts, news/suggestions/puzzle, and lower-right conversation. At ≤760px it becomes a distinct full-width feed with 56px compact header and fixed five-position navigation—do not compress desktop rails into mobile.

Search, sort, composer/publish, reactions, repost/comment initiation, connects and local messages alter local state. Six authored feed entries load initially and six deterministic records append within 920px of document end; search/sort reset to six. Source records contain 20 posts across 18 people/organisations; keys are source ID + batch, never display text. Jobs begins at 24 and appends batches of 24 through 1,000 static records; mobile has a full-height detail/back transition.

Keep compact metadata, contextual surfacing, social proof and Like/Comment/Repost/Send actions; public Unsplash media is density material, not a claim about real members. Models live in `components/ui/sns/linkedin/1/model`, interactive screen/icons/CSS in `screen`. The research basis was 56 current records (45 official LinkedIn Help pages plus 11 captures), especially [homepage](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a523215/homepage-overview), [feed](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a523360/linkedin-feed-overview), [messaging](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a569449), [sort](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1480504), [posts](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a528176), and [jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/topic/a153003?lang=en).
