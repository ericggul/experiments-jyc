# LinkedIn 5

Experimental local fork at `/sns/linkedin/5`, created 2026-09-13 from the
complete `/sns/linkedin/2` reconstruction. The starting layout, content,
assets, local controls, and route-specific views remain the baseline.

The tested relation is pointer-led abstraction. First contact selects only the
outermost eligible component under the pointer and places one opaque colour
plane over it. Later contact with a particular descendant selects only that
descendant, giving it a higher local `z-index` and revealing its own colour
plane above the parent. No click, hover control, or mode switch is required;
the composition accumulates only through this actual pointer traversal.

The changed variable is the trigger, granularity, and local stacking of the
coloured-block treatment. Original component geometry and local action handlers
remain in place, but a treated region deliberately no longer exposes its source
content. This has not been assessed for task completion or touch interaction. A
later fork can test whether a short-lived rather than cumulative trace better
exposes the feed's reading path.

The `options` panel follows the compact English control grammar used by the
Goldfishes overlay-4 field. `colour` restores the ten-colour treatment;
`monochrome` maps every existing and future colour plane into a grey family;
`line layout` retains a white surface but waits for the same pointer-led
selection: each contacted component replaces its own colour plane with a white
plane and black straight outline. The panel itself is excluded from pointer
abstraction so these alternatives remain reachable.
