# Instagram mobile — /sns/mobile/11

2026-09-23. An editable, reference-informed Instagram mobile reconstruction for moving through a dense social feed. The prior nine-post sample stopped after five feed photos and placed Create in the center of the old five-tab bar. This revision uses Home, Reels, Messages, Search and Profile, with Create in the Home header. The 100 photo posts are all reachable by feed scroll and Explore; accounts alternate in the feed. Reels, profile grids, stories, comments, saves, follows, local posting, the share sheet, and post options continue to connect through one local model. Messages now opens a local thread, and sharing a post adds it to the chosen thread. Activity opens from the Home heart icon. The Reels Friends choice shows the boundary of this preview: there is no real friend activity to populate it.

The initial inventory contains **100 photo posts with 100 different Unsplash source photo IDs and 100 different captions**, plus two bundled video Reels with separate thumbnails. This count excludes new posts that participants add during a session. The photos were selected by subject from public Unsplash search pages and given image-specific descriptions; the account identities, social relations, locations, post text, comments and engagement numbers are fictional. The feed includes personal updates, a shop announcement, conversation prompts, varied caption lengths, and selected comments. Photo IDs are distinct; re-crops or reordered repeats are not counted as new posts. External photo delivery depends on Unsplash.

## Reference boundary

- [Meta's Instagram navigation announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/in-india-instagram-debuts-a-reels-first-experience-for-its-mobile-app/) (28 September 2025) explicitly announces DMs moving to the center of the navigation bar and Reels moving to the second tab, with swiping between tabs. The same note describes Reels-first landing as a **limited India test**. This experiment retains Home as its initial screen; it does not treat the India test as a worldwide default. The announcement establishes intended navigation roles, not a verified 2026 global rollout or exact pixel geometry.
- [Meta's repost and Reels Friends announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/) (6 August 2025) establishes Reels/Friends as separate views and a profile repost tab. Here the Friends view remains empty because the fictional accounts have no evidenced friend activity. Reposts are not simulated.
- [Instagram's official App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/instagram/id389801252) confirms the continuing Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore, messaging and profile feature set. It does not establish a signed-in screen state or exact navigation geometry.
- The 100 still images and their alt descriptions were selected from public Unsplash searches for [street photography](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/street-photography), [interiors](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/interior-design), [flowers](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/flowers), [cafes](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/cafe), [books](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/books), [architecture](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/architecture), [landscape](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/landscape), [portraits](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/portrait-photography), [food](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/food-photography) and [fashion](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/fashion-photography). These are content sources, not Instagram interface evidence.

Source mode: reference-informed editable reconstruction. No authenticated Instagram archive or same-viewport source frame was available. The source evidence supports the navigation roles and main feature families; spacing, icon drawings, account content, local activity, message handling and secondary sheets are experiment adaptations. The share and message actions stay inside this mounted session. Posting uses eight sample images; it does not upload a file or publish to Instagram.

Static verification on 2026-09-23: 100 photo entries, 100 unique source IDs, 100 unique captions; SCC TypeScript check, scoped ESLint and diff check passed. Repository policy did not authorize a browser or server check, so rendered geometry, actual image availability, touch paths, autoplay and visual parity remain unverified. This is not a pixel-fidelity claim.
