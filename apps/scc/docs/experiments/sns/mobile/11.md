# Instagram mobile — /sns/mobile/11

2026-09-23. An editable, reference-informed Instagram mobile reconstruction for moving through a dense social feed. The prior nine-post sample stopped after five feed photos and placed Create in the center of the old five-tab bar. This revision uses Home, Reels, Messages, Search and Profile. The current visual pass puts Create at the left of the centered wordmark, Activity at the right, and the five destinations in a floating translucent pill, following Instagram's current US iPhone listing. Post actions now include visible counts and a session-only repost toggle. The 100 photo posts are all reachable by feed scroll and Explore; accounts alternate in the feed. Reels, profile grids, stories, comments, saves, follows, local posting, the share sheet, and post options continue to connect through one local model. Messages now opens a local thread, and sharing a post adds it to the chosen thread. The Reels Friends choice shows the boundary of this preview: there is no real friend activity to populate it.

The initial inventory contains **100 photo posts with 100 different Unsplash source photo IDs and 100 different captions**, plus two bundled video Reels with separate thumbnails. This count excludes new posts that participants add during a session. The photos were selected by subject from public Unsplash search pages and given image-specific descriptions; the account identities, social relations, locations, post text, comments and engagement numbers are fictional. The feed includes personal updates, a shop announcement, conversation prompts, varied caption lengths, and selected comments. Photo IDs are distinct; re-crops or reordered repeats are not counted as new posts. External photo delivery depends on Unsplash.

## Reference boundary

- [Meta's Instagram navigation announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/in-india-instagram-debuts-a-reels-first-experience-for-its-mobile-app/) (28 September 2025) explicitly announces DMs moving to the center of the navigation bar and Reels moving to the second tab, with swiping between tabs. The same note describes Reels-first landing as a **limited India test**. This experiment retains Home as its initial screen; it does not treat the India test as a worldwide default. The announcement establishes intended navigation roles, not a verified 2026 global rollout or exact pixel geometry.
- [Meta's repost and Reels Friends announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/) (6 August 2025) establishes Reels/Friends as separate views and reposts. Here the Friends view remains empty because the fictional accounts have no evidenced friend activity. Reposting toggles only the visible local post state; it does not distribute a post to another account's profile.

Source mode: reference-informed editable reconstruction. The App Store Home frame supplies a current 430 px-wide iPhone composition but does not expose CSS, device scale, account flags, or all interaction states. This pass follows its visible header order, post action grammar, feed density, and floating nav, while using the separately announced 2026 wordmark. Icon drawings, account content, generated engagement counts, local activity, message handling and secondary sheets are experiment adaptations. The share, repost and message actions stay inside this mounted session. Posting uses eight sample images; it does not upload a file or publish to Instagram.
