# X mobile surface — `/sns/mobile/13`

Updated 2026-09-23. This is a local, fictional X interface study for the SNS collage archive. The tested path is feed → post and replies → author profile → another post, with a return path through Back. The prior `/13` implementation had eight top-level seed posts and a light mobile shell. This revision keeps its independent route and session-only state while expanding the timeline. The 2026-09-23 visual pass tightened the shell, icons, and post rows after the local route was found too large and loose relative to X.

The For you and Following tabs filter the feed; Following includes accounts followed in the local session. Search has Top (matched posts by likes), Latest (relative-time order), and People (matching names, handles, and bios); trend taps populate the search. A post opens its detail, including a quoted post and authored reply chains. Author identity opens a profile with Posts, Replies, and Media; the local account also has Likes. Reply and compose create session posts. Like, repost, bookmark, and follow have visible state. The account menu opens profile and bookmarks. The bottom Communities tab switches among three local topical streams; Messages opens three fictional conversations and accepts session-only replies. These two screens are local authored interactions, not claims of X network or ranking behavior.

The seed contains **108 top-level posts**: 100 newly written entries plus eight retained posts, with **100 distinct normalized new texts across 20 fictional accounts**. It also contains **14 authored replies**, including replies to replies. Seven of the new posts have images; the retained set has four image posts. Media uses eight distinct Unsplash photo URLs with alt text and remains an external load dependency. The writing mixes local reporting, sport, transport, food, software, books, music, gardening, weather, games, questions, correction, and short reactions. The quoted post and linked reply chains let the feed lead into conversation instead of repeating isolated cards. Counts and relative times are fictional local fixture values.

## Reference boundary and visual trial

The primary visual state is the [X iPhone Following feed reproduced in Harian Terbit's 2026-03-17 article](https://www.harianterbit.com/humaniora/27416881784/platform-x-batasi-usia-pengguna-di-indonesia-mulai-27-maret-akun-di-bawah-16-tahun-bakal-diblokir) ([image](https://static.promediateknologi.id/crop/0x0%3A0x0/1200x600/webp/photo/p1/274/2026/03/17/harian-terbit-x-737970442.jpg)): English, signed-in appearance, dark theme, Following selected, with a February 17 2026 post visible. The publication date is known; the original app capture date and device scale are not. It is a composited and cropped press image, so the following are visual estimates rather than extracted app CSS: near-black background, a thin centered X mark around 26 px, avatar around 40 px, 14–15 px feed text, 16–18 px action glyphs, roughly 10 px post inset, and a 2 px blue tab underline. The target omits the pictured iPhone's hardware and status chrome.

The primary frame does not show the bottom bar. The [public App-Liv X feed image](https://app-liv.jp/2/) and the [official X App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/x/id333903271), checked 2026-09-23, provide corroboration for the five evenly spaced bottom destinations and compact outline icon grammar. Those sources are undated UI frames and may reflect different account flags or versions; the bottom bar is an informed reconstruction, not an exact measurement of the March press frame. The App Store promotional screenshots also show detail typography and actions but are tilted composites, so they were not used as geometry oracles. [X Help: For you timeline](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-timeline) supports the feed tabs, post-detail path, and bookmarks in the navigation menu; [X Help: profile media timeline](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/view-all-x-pictures) supports a profile Media tab. Neither documents pixel dimensions.

This pass replaced the typographic X character with a vector mark, redrew the navigation and post action glyphs, made the five bottom slots equal with 24 px icons, reduced the tab underline to 2 px, tightened the header and post spacing, and kept the 40 px post avatar and 15 px body text. It added a working people-finding header action, leading to the local Explore screen. The 108 top-level posts, 14 authored replies, and existing session interactions remain intact.

No local browser or runtime check was authorized. Exact rendered geometry, responsive behavior, image loading, and interaction timing remain unverified; the UIForge visual gate is pending. Scoped ESLint and a focused TypeScript check of `/13` passed; the repository-wide typecheck is currently blocked by syntax errors in generated `.next/dev/types/routes.d.ts`. A static fixture audit still finds 100 new top-level posts, eight retained top-level posts, and 14 authored replies. The source image is partial and cannot establish a universal 2026 X layout or behavior for unseen screens.
