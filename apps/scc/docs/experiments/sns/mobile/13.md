# X mobile surface — `/sns/mobile/13`

Updated 2026-10-11. This is a local, fictional X (iOS, dark theme) interface study for the SNS collage archive. All state is session-only. The tested path is feed → post and replies → author profile → another post, with Back restoring the previous screen and its scroll position.

## Behavior

Home has the 2026 timeline strip: **For you ⌄**, Following, three pinned topic timelines (Cities, Culture, Everyday — the former Communities streams), and **+**. Tapping the active For you or Following tab opens its sheet; Following can sort by Recent or Popular. The header (account avatar, centred X mark) and tabs slide away when scrolling down and return on scroll up. While they are hidden, the tab bar and FAB fade.

The tab bar has Home, Search, Grok, Notifications (with an unread badge until visited), and Chat. Tapping the current tab scrolls it to the top. Communities moved to the side drawer after X [deprecated Communities in May 2026](https://hyper.ai/en/stories/debdd6f718b8375ff353db3c50810a76). Messages are labelled Chat after the [DM–XChat merge](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-129/Technology/X-Unifies-Its-Messaging-System-Merging-Direct-Messages-with-XChat-into-a-Single-Tab-47350).

- **Posts.** Tapping anywhere on a post opens its detail; avatar or name opens the profile. Like fills the heart with a pop, ring, and count roll. Repost opens a Repost/Quote sheet, and Quote opens the composer with the quoted card. Share opens Copy link / Send via Chat / Bookmark. The ⋯ menu opens a sheet: Follow/Unfollow works, and your own posts can be deleted.
- **Detail.** Shows the parent post joined by a thread line, plus the full timestamp and Views. The action row includes a bookmark count. It also has Relevant and View quotes controls, replies with first-child thread chains, and a fixed “Post your reply” bar.
- **FAB.** The first tap opens the Go Live / Spaces / Photos speed dial; the second tap (feather) opens the full-screen composer. The composer has Cancel, Drafts, and Post, plus “Everyone can reply”, the media/GIF/Grok/poll/emoji/location tools, a character ring, and add-post.
- **Drawer.** The avatar opens a left drawer with Profile, Premium, Bookmarks, Lists, Communities, History, and Settings and privacy.
- **Explore.** Shows the search pill and For You / Trending / News / Sports / Entertainment. Typing shows people typeahead. Submitting, or tapping a trend, gives Top / Latest / People / Media results.
- **Notifications.** All / Verified / Mentions.
- **Chat.** A conversation list, then threads with blue sent and grey received bubbles.
- **Grok.** A local screen that states it is not connected; no model is called.
- **Profiles.** A banner photo with glass buttons, an 80 px avatar overlapping the banner, Follow/Follow back plus message and notify, a “Follows you” chip, location and join date, counts, and “Followed by”. Profile tabs are icons, and the active tab also shows its label.

## 2026-10-11 fidelity pass

**Divergences found before the pass (390 px check):**

1. Avatars were initials, and 14 of 20 accounts had no colour class, so they rendered as bare text.
2. The bottom bar showed Communities and an envelope instead of Grok and Chat.
3. The header had a non-standard “find people” icon.
4. The tab underline was a short blue pill; tabs were not scrollable and had no pinned timelines.
5. The action row had loose, uneven spacing, showed zero counts, and glyph sizes did not match.
6. The FAB had no speed dial.
7. The account menu was a full page, not a drawer.
8. There were no share/repost/more sheets, and no like animation.
9. The header did not hide on scroll.
10. Detail lacked the parent thread, a full timestamp, and the reply bar.
11. Profiles had flat colour banners and text tabs.
12. Notifications used text glyphs (♥ ✦ ↻).
13. Messages and composer layouts did not match.
14. The font was Arial.

The “only ~3 posts” observation was the viewport, not the data: all 108 top-level posts render in the scroller (17,605 px tall).

**Changed:** `model/data.ts` now holds photo avatars and banners, typed verification, join dates, and trend IDs. `screen/app.tsx`, `screen/app.module.css`, `screen/icons.tsx`, and `screen/format.ts` replace `screen/index.tsx` and `x.module.css`, and they implement everything listed above. Closed overlays are `visibility: hidden` and `inert`.

**Remaining differences:**

- Chirp is not bundled; the system SF/Segoe stack stands in.
- The tab-bar order (Home, Search, Grok, Notifications, Chat) is reconstructed from reporting and the web nav, not from a dated iOS capture.
- The profile icon tabs follow the App Store frame, which may be a staged build.
- Sheet contents, the speed-dial items, and the Grok screen are plausible reconstructions. Unavailable actions show a local toast.
- No pull-to-refresh, “new posts” pill, video, Spaces, or live data.
- Images remain an external Unsplash dependency.
