# X mobile surface — `/sns/mobile/13`

Updated 2026-09-23. This is a local, fictional X interface study for the SNS collage archive. The tested path is feed → post and replies → author profile → another post, with a return path through Back. The prior `/13` implementation had eight top-level seed posts and a light mobile shell. This revision keeps its independent route and session-only state while expanding the timeline and revising the shell against public X material.

The For you and Following tabs filter the feed; Following includes accounts followed in the local session. Search has Top (matched posts by likes), Latest (relative-time order), and People (matching names, handles, and bios); trend taps populate the search. A post opens its detail, including a quoted post and authored reply chains. Author identity opens a profile with Posts, Replies, and Media; the local account also has Likes. Reply and compose create session posts. Like, repost, bookmark, and follow have visible state. The account menu opens profile and bookmarks. The bottom Communities tab switches among three local topical streams; Messages opens three fictional conversations and accepts session-only replies. These two screens are local authored interactions, not claims of X network or ranking behavior.

The seed contains **108 top-level posts**: 100 newly written entries plus eight retained posts, with **100 distinct normalized new texts across 20 fictional accounts**. It also contains **14 authored replies**, including replies to replies. Seven of the new posts have images; the retained set has four image posts. Media uses eight distinct Unsplash photo URLs with alt text and remains an external load dependency. The writing mixes local reporting, sport, transport, food, software, books, music, gardening, weather, games, questions, correction, and short reactions. The quoted post and linked reply chains let the feed lead into conversation instead of repeating isolated cards. Counts and relative times are fictional local fixture values.
