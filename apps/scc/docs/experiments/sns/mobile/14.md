# YouTube mobile — /sns/mobile/14

Experimental, source-informed reproduction of the current YouTube iOS app, made 2026-10-11. What sets it apart from the other SNS clones is the **continuous watch ⇄ miniplayer passage**. One `<video>` element plays through a drag that shrinks the watch page into a bar docked above the bottom nav and expands it again. Around that passage sit the Home feed, Shorts, Subscriptions, You, Search, Notifications, channel pages and playlists. The earlier desktop/mobile study in `components/ui/sns/youtube/2/` was a starting point for the data and icon vocabulary only; layout, interaction and media are new. Code: `components/mobile/clone/14/` (`screen/index.tsx`, `screen/icons.tsx`, `screen/youtube.module.css`, `model/{data,format,gesture,feed}.ts`, `model/model.test.mjs`).

## Reference boundary

- The five iPhone frames of the [YouTube App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/youtube-watch-listen-stream/id544007664) (Create sheet over Home, Shorts player, Watch page, playlist panel, Home feed) were downloaded and read. They are the visual reference for the top bar (logo, cast, bell, search), the chip row with Explore compass first, full-bleed 16:9 cards with duration badges, the Shorts shelf, the watch page (title, channel row with Subscribe pill, action pills, comments card, full-width up-next cards), the Shorts rail and the Create sheet items. These frames show an older "Library" tab, so the bottom nav uses the current Home / Shorts / + / Subscriptions / You set.
- Inner surfaces that the listing does not show are semantic reconstructions: the notification and settings sheets, Subscribed bell options with the unsubscribe confirm, the Save-to-playlist sheet, the share panel, search suggestions and results, the notification list, the You page, playlist pages and the channel page. No pixel parity is claimed for them.
- All channels, people, titles, counts, comments and posts are fictional. No real YouTube channel is impersonated. The brand mark is the red play glyph plus a text wordmark.

## Media and licences

- **20 horizontal watch videos** are freely licensed Wikimedia Commons files. Each plays its 480p VP9 WebM transcode from `upload.wikimedia.org` (switchable to 720p/360p/240p), and its poster is the Commons 960px frame. Each video's description and its Description panel credit the original file, author and licence, and link to the Commons page. The licences are CC BY 3.0/4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 and public domain. Exact files: `model/data.ts`. They were picked through the [Commons search API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Search) to be horizontal, 15 s to 3.5 min long, and to have a 480p transcode.
- **10 Shorts.** Five are the repository's local portrait clips (`public/video/videos/*.mp4`, the same clips clone 12 uses), with posters copied into `clone/14/assets/`. Five are portrait Commons files (chick hatching, crème brûlée, NASA SVS exoplanets, seagull, wood cleaving), credited by author on the Short.
- Avatars: each channel uses a frame from its own footage. Commenter and profile photos come from Unsplash.
- Remote playback depends on Commons availability, network access and the browser's VP9 WebM support (older iOS Safari versions lack it).

## Interaction contract

- **Home**: the header (top bar and chips) follows scroll. It hides as you scroll down, comes back as you scroll up, and settles to fully shown or fully hidden. Chips filter by topic, plus "Recently uploaded" and "New to you". Shorts shelves sit after the 1st and 7th video. A red bar under a thumbnail shows watched progress. The card kebab opens a sheet: Save to Watch later, Save to playlist, Download, Share, Not interested (a "Video removed" placeholder with Undo), Don't recommend channel (with Undo). The Explore compass opens destinations (Trending, Music, Learning …).
- **Watch**: tap shows or hides the controls, which auto-hide after 3 s. Controls: minimise chevron, autoplay switch, cast, CC, settings (quality, speed, loop), previous/play/next, time, fullscreen. **Double-tap** on the left or right 40 % seeks ±10 s with a ripple, and repeated taps add up (10, 20, 30 s). The red scrubber can be dragged and shows buffered range and a time tip. When a video ends with autoplay on, a 5 s "Up next" card appears with Cancel / Play now. Below the player: the title and meta open the Description panel (stats, text, Commons source), then the channel row with Subscribe or Subscribed+bell, then the action pills (like|dislike, Share, Remix, Download with a progress ring, Save), the comments card that opens the Comments panel, and up-next cards. Swiping up on the player enters fullscreen and swiping down leaves it.
- **Miniplayer**: dragging the player down maps pointer distance to one progress value `--p` (0 → 1). Player size, page offset, body fade, mini-bar fade and bottom-nav slide are all derived from it in CSS, so the drag is continuous and does not re-render React. On release the player snaps by flick velocity, or by distance past half (`model/gesture.ts`). The mini bar keeps playing. Tap or drag up expands it, ✕ closes it. Opening any video expands to the full page. On the Shorts tab the miniplayer pauses and hides.
- **Shorts**: a vertical pager driven by pointer drag (18 % distance or a flick moves one item; the ends rubber-band), wheel and arrow keys. Tap pauses, double-tap likes with a burst. The rail has like, dislike, comments (bottom sheet), share, remix and a sound tile (mute toggle). Channel and Subscribe sit bottom-left with the caption and sound. The progress line seeks. If sound autoplay is refused, the Short plays muted with "Tap to unmute".
- **Create**: a Short (or a Remix: Sound, Green screen, Cut, Collab) and Upload a video each take a local video file through a file input. The Short is added to the front of the Shorts feed; the video goes to You › Your videos and is playable. Create a post publishes to your channel's Posts. Go live shows a notice only.
- **Subscriptions**: a row of subscribed-channel avatars (blue dot for unwatched uploads; tapping one filters to that channel), chips All / Today / Videos / Shorts / Continue watching / Unwatched, and community posts in the feed. **You**: profile, Incognito (pauses history), History shelf, playlists (Watch later, Liked videos, user playlists, + to create one), Your videos, Downloads, Time watched, Settings (autoplay, incognito, clear watch/search history). **Search**: history and trending rows, live suggestions with the match bolded, ↖ to fill the field, voice search where the Web Speech API exists, and results with a channel card, a Shorts shelf and chips. **Notifications**: New and Earlier groups. Opening the list clears the bell badge, and a blue dot stays on an item until it is opened.
- **Session state** is kept in `sessionStorage` and survives a reload in the same tab: likes, dislikes, subscriptions and bell levels, Watch later, playlists, history, resume positions, downloads, comments and comment likes, posts, hidden videos, blocked channels, search history, incognito, autoplay. The open player and uploaded files (object URLs) last only for the page's lifetime.
- **Transforms**: plain DOM inside `<main>`, inline SVG icons, `<img>`/`<video>` media, and sheets/dialogs as absolute overlays with a full backdrop. Visible strings are JSX text or model fields (`title`, `name`, `label`, `views`, `time`) and `setToast` calls in `screen/index.tsx` and `model/*.ts`; `language/tools/extract.mjs` finds 268 strings for clone 14.

## Verification (2026-10-11)

- `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit -p .`: no clone/14 errors. `pnpm exec eslint components/mobile/clone/14`: clean. `node --test model/model.test.mjs`: 6/6 pass (counts and clocks, resume, miniplayer snap, pager step and rubber-band, header offset and settle, double-tap seek accumulation, scrub mapping, up-next ordering, suggestions).
- Browser check: Chrome automation tab, route in a 390 px iframe, plus 320 px and full-desktop width. Confirmed:
  - Home layout matches the reference frames, and the chips and the desktop centred 440 px column look right.
  - Opening a video works. A real pointer drag collapsed it to the miniplayer, and a drag up expanded it again. A held mid-drag at `--p≈0.37` showed the shrinking player over the page.
  - A double-tap produced the ripple, and a third tap added up to "20 seconds", with the red progress moving.
  - Comments panel: a comment was posted and showed under Newest.
  - Unsubscribe confirm, then Subscribe (with toast), the Save sheet's Watch later checkbox and the Description panel with its Commons source all worked.
  - Fullscreen toggles. Quality switched the source to the 360p transcode.
  - Shorts: a real swipe moved to the next item, and a double-tap liked with a burst. The comments sheet opened.
  - Search suggestions for "volc" and the results appeared. The You, Subscriptions, Notifications, Create sheet and channel pages rendered.
  - Session state survived a reload. There is no horizontal overflow at 320 px.
- **Not verified**: actual playback, timeupdate-driven progress, resume, autoplay countdown and the download ring's timing. The automation tab was hidden, so Chrome loaded no media (readyState stayed 0, even for local MP4s) and throttled timers to 1 s. Both Commons and local URLs did answer `206` to range requests from the page. Real iPhone Safari, safe-area insets, file pickers, `navigator.share` and speech input were not tested.

## Known gaps

- Fullscreen is a full-column overlay; the player is not rotated to landscape.
- Captions, casting and live streaming are explanatory notices only. Quality options for local MP4 Shorts are Auto only. Seeded comment replies are generic.
- No recommendation model: Home is a fixed order filtered by chip. Up next ranks same-channel first, then shared topics.
- The miniplayer cannot be swiped sideways to dismiss. The comments and description panels close by ✕ (global sheets also close by dragging the grabber).
