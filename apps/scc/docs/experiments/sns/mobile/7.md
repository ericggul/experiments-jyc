# SIDEWALK — mobile/7

2026-09-23. Independent fictional New York food-ordering surface at `/sns/mobile/7`; preserves mobile/1–5. Built from the functional scope of mobile/2, with a new US ordering composition, dollar prices, NYC neighborhoods, menu customization and explicit tipping. Components own their data and CSS.

The primary job is to choose a nearby restaurant, build one restaurant's bag and review the full cost. Location, delivery/pickup, search, cuisine, speed and sort controls precede food photography. Restaurant names, ratings, delivery fees and timing sit directly beneath large crops; no marketing hero or fake device chrome. Red marks the ordering action, while most UI remains dense black on white.

## Visual source

Inspected the actual DoorDash US mobile screenshot reproduced in [CouponFollow's delivery comparison](https://couponfollow.com/research/doordash-vs-grubhub-vs-ubereats), [direct image](https://images.ctfassets.net/hqqw1qsaw5d8/6ae446c883ea4e7aa3f39ed67bfc29f0/2f68e70ba731a53393fdb02c026f6a0e/image.png), before implementation. The 778-pixel-wide reference aperture shows a location-first header, horizontal category/filter controls, approximately 30-pixel edge margins, bold compact headings, food images with external metadata, red active navigation and anchored bottom tabs. SIDEWALK adapts this operational grammar to a fictional service; it is not a pixel replica and does not reproduce promotional claims. Food photos load from Unsplash URLs recorded directly in `model/data.ts`; remote availability remains a dependency.

## Behavior

Five fictional restaurants have distinct menus. Search includes dishes and neighborhoods; cuisine, 30-minutes-or-less and sort controls compose. Saved restaurants, restaurant details, add-ons, special instructions, bounded quantities, removal, and explicit cross-restaurant replacement work in local state. Delivery/pickup and editable addresses carry into checkout. Totals disclose subtotal, delivery, service, estimated tax and selected percentage tip. Checkout explicitly states simulation and records a local order with items, destination and fulfillment instructions; no payment or network ordering exists. State lasts for the mounted visit.

Native dialogs provide focus containment and Escape dismissal. Stable model IDs key all repeated entities. Layout is constrained to 480px, uses safe areas and internal scrolling, and includes 320px adjustments. Scoped ESLint passed. Static checks only: browser/runtime verification was not authorized, so imagery loading, rendering and device interaction have not been observed.
