# SNS YouTube 3

Route: `/sns/youtube/3`

## Inverted viewport scale — 2026-08-27

- **Baseline:** `/sns/youtube/2`, rendered unchanged inside a same-origin frame.
- **Changed variable:** the surrounding device always supplies the opposite fixed
  layout viewport: a phone holds a `1920 × 1080` desktop interface, while a
  desktop holds a `390 × 844` phone interface. The source surface is scaled
  independently on its horizontal and vertical axes until it occupies the
  surrounding viewport completely: no reflow, cropping, or letterbox.
- **Invariant:** YouTube 2's implementation, visual grammar, data, navigation,
  and local interactions are not changed. Its original route continues to
  render at its native responsive breakpoints.
- **Participant situation:** someone confronts the habitual device interface at
  the wrong physical scale: a desktop compressed into a phone, or a phone feed
  stretched laterally across a laptop.
- **Perceptual job:** keep the source interface recognisable while making its
  assumed device proportion perceptible through its deliberate distortion.
- **Interaction job:** every control remains the original live control, simply
  transformed with its surrounding interface.
- **Observed question:** does the lost convenience make the separate grammar of
  desktop scanning and phone continuation more apparent, or merely distant?
