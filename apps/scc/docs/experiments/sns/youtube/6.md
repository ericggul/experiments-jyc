# SNS YouTube 6

Route: `/sns/youtube/6`

## Eight-column content density — 2026-08-27

- **Baseline:** `/sns/youtube/2` at its ordinary responsive breakpoint.
- **Changed variable:** the desktop guide and header remain a single native
  interface. The right content field is a real eight-column grid of individual
  cards, not a repeated screenshot or iframe field. Each card uses the original
  YouTube record, thumbnail, duration, avatar, title, and metadata at one half
  of its normal desktop card scale; its row rhythm is reduced by the same ratio.
  On mobile, the native header and bottom navigation remain singular while the
  body becomes an equivalently dense two-column card grid.
- **Invariant:** the YouTube 2 source data, visual language, and responsive
  breakpoint conventions are preserved without changing its implementation.
  YouTube 3–5 are also unchanged.
- **Participant situation:** a familiar browsing frame retains its normal
  command surface while its recommendation field becomes overpopulated.
- **Perceptual job:** preserve the guide/content distinction and make the
  altered density legible without a second dashboard wrapper.
- **Interaction job:** the guide/header, topics, search, navigation state, and
  individual video controls remain real interface elements.
