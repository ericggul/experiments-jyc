# Road signs / direction / 2

`/transportation/road-signs/direction/2`, 2026-10-07: a recursive tree of
three-way instruction signs. Every sign points 직진, 좌회전 and 우회전 from
one stem; beyond each arrow sits a smaller sign turned to that arrow's
heading, which again points straight, left and right.

- **Baseline:** [`/direction/1`](1.md)'s arrow construction (305 stem and
  head, 306/307 bend of radius 195), copied into this route's model so the
  two trials stay independent. Recursive placement follows the fractal
  [clock](../../../../complex-systems/fractal/clock/README.md) and
  [logo](../../../../complex-systems/fractal/logo/1.md): copies at tips,
  laid out breadth-first, cut at sub-pixel size or a sprite budget.
- **Changed variable:** one sign becomes a ternary tree. A child's radius is
  0.45 of its parent's, with a 0.06-radius gap between rims. Every sign is
  the original blue #1e50a3 field with white arrows. Parents are drawn over
  their children.
- **Removed 2026-10-07:** the first version coloured each disc by the turn
  that led to it: straight blue, left red, right yellow with a black arrow.
  That colouring was not asked for, so it was removed.
- **Interaction:** the pointer's angle off screen-up, on either side, sets the
  shared left/right turn angle, clamped to 20°–160° (90° at load). The whole
  tree re-forms as it follows, smoothed. The tree's bounding box is fitted to
  94% of the viewport.
- **Rendering:** Canvas 2D, up to 6000 signs (about 1100 at 1600 × 1000).
  Discs under 1.5 px are skipped and under 4 px are drawn without arrows. One
  Path2D per frame is shared by every sign. It redraws only while the angle
  settles or on resize.
- **Material:** flat sheeting, white border and a grey rim edge on the overcast
  sky of `/1`, without weathering or reflections.

Checked on 2026-10-07 without a browser: the layout was rasterised at 45°, 90°
and 140° with qlmanage. At wide angles, the left and right subtrees fold back
over their ancestors, so deep signs overlap. Scoped lint, TypeScript and the
navigation test pass. Frame time and the live interaction are unmeasured.
