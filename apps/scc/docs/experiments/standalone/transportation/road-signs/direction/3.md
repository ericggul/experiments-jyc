# Road signs / direction / 3

`/transportation/road-signs/direction/3`, 2026-10-07: one 지시표지 filling the
screen, like [`/1`](1.md), whose single connected arrow branches without end.
The stem splits into 직진, 좌회전 and 우회전 (north, west and east of its own
heading). Each branch, straight from its own end, splits again into straight,
left and right, and so on. There is no head or gap between levels. Only the
last level ends in arrowheads.

- **Baseline:** [`/2`](2.md) recursed whole signs, which read as "a sign, then
  a sign, then a sign". `/2` is kept as it was.
- **Changed variable:** the recursion lives inside one arrow. Branches come
  from the `/1` construction (305 stem and head, 306/307 bend of radius 195,
  shaft 132), copied into this route. Each split's branches have half the
  length scale of their parent, and they taper from the parent's end width to
  half of it. Every split therefore flows out of the stem like 310, with no
  steps in width. The first split keeps the stem's length scale. The arrow is
  centred on its bounding box and scaled to reach at most 440 units from the
  face centre.
- **Colour:** the original white arrow on the #1e50a3 field, with the white
  border, grey rim, post and overcast sky of `/1`. No other colour.
- **Interaction:** the pointer's angle off screen-up, on either side, sets the
  shared turn angle of every split, clamped to 60°–160° (90° at load), with
  smoothing. The pointer is ignored over the options panel.
- **Options** (옵션/닫기, the plain-text grammar of traffic-light/2, copied
  here):
  - 모드: 정지 (default) or 성장.
  - 성장 시간: 2–20 s, default 7.
  - 각도: synced with the pointer.
  - 길이 배율: 0.3–0.7, default 0.5.
  - 굵기 배율: 0.3–0.8, default 0.5.
  - 단계: 1–7. 단계 자동 returns to the automatic depth: the deepest level
    whose leaf shafts stay 3 device px wide at 90°. It is fixed while the angle
    moves, so it never pops.
  - 크기: sign diameter, 40–100 vmin, default 96 (raised from 88 on request).
- **성장 mode:** the arrow grows like a tree, not level by level. Growth is a
  distance from the stem foot, eased once over the whole cycle. Every tip
  advances at the same speed, and a split's branches start the moment their
  parent ends. The straight branch carries its parent's head on. The left and
  right heads grow from nothing over the first 45% of their branch, so a split
  reads as one arrow sprouting two side arrows rather than three stacked
  heads. The cycle is grow, hold 1.6 s, withdraw, hold 0.8 s.
- **Rendering:** the whole arrow is one Path2D of tapered branch outlines and
  leaf heads, all wound the same way and filled once with the nonzero rule.
  Overlaps merge and joints show no seams. It is clipped to the field. In 정지
  mode it redraws only while the angle settles, on resize or on an option
  change; in 성장 mode it redraws every frame. Bends are sampled about 3 device
  px apart, so deep branches cost few points.

**Failures on the way, 2026-10-07:**

- The first `/3` placed a smaller separate three-way arrow after a gap past
  each head. Its arrows were disconnected, not one branching arrow.
- The first connected draft stroked each branch at a constant width. That
  gave square shoulders where widths stepped down, and hairline seams between
  strokes.
- A winding mismatch between heads and shafts punched pinholes where they
  overlapped.

- In the browser, building a Path2D took about 1.7 s at depth 8 with fixed
  bend sampling (about 400k calls, measured while the window was hidden).
  Depth is now capped at 7 and sampling adapts to pixel size: about 70k calls
  at depth 7 and 7k at the default depth 5.
- An earlier reading of "0.5 fps" in grow mode was Chrome pausing frames for a
  hidden window (`visibilityState: hidden`), not drawing cost. A CPU-canvas
  switch made on that reading was reverted.
