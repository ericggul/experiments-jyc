# Road signs / direction / 4

`/transportation/road-signs/direction/4`, 2026-10-07: a full-viewport grid of
지시표지 played by finger skating. Also listed as
[`/mobile/finger-skating/road-sign/1`](../../../../mobile/finger-skating.md);
that row opens this route, and the code stays here.

- **Baseline:** [`/1`](1.md)'s 305–307 arrow (`arrowFor`) and plate colours,
  on its sky gradient, laid out like
  [`fractal/clock/2`](../../../../complex-systems/fractal/clock/README.md) but
  denser: 36 px mobile cells, up to
  52 px on desktop, 20 cells on the short side, plate radius 0.47 of a cell,
  headings carried across resizes.
- **Changed variable:** the clock's two hands become one arrow. In opt 1, `exit`, a sign turns its arrow toward the point where the finger left its
  rim. `follow` also turns every arrow toward the
  nearest finger while it is down, as clock/2's minute hands do. Headings stay
  after release; the spring is clock/2's (stiffness 70, damping ratio 0.72).
- **Default:** opt 2. Choosing an option resets every sign (upright, straight,
  no recorded exit) and starts again.
- **Hybrid modes:** each sign also has a whole-sign rotation, upright in
  opt 1 (`exit`) and opt 2 (`follow`). opt 3 bends the arrow by exit while
  the sign turns toward the nearest finger; opt 4 starts from the finger: the sign
  already faces the finger, so at the rim the exit point is almost always
  straight ahead in the sign's frame and says nothing. What the finger does
  there is travel in a direction, so opt 4 records that travel heading (over
  the last 12 px of path) when it leaves the rim, and keeps the drawn arrow
  (rotation + bend) on it while the sign goes on following. The bend is
  locked to the rotation each frame rather than sprung, so it never lags; a
  new exit or a change of side springs there first. A bend that reaches a
  U-turn holds there until the sign turns 0.6 rad further, then sweeps through
  straight to the other side. Earlier opt 4 attempts on 2026-10-07 used the
  exit point (subtracted once, then re-solved through a spring) and pointed
  nearly straight or lagged. Otherwise both angles are
  measured from screen up and the arrow is drawn at rotation + bend; the sign
  rotates the shorter way. An `arrow follow · sign exit` option (opt 5) was tried and removed
  on 2026-10-07; the model's `sign` exit channel remains.
- **Turning:** a heading is the arrow's bend in [−π, π], never wrapped: −π is
  the left U-turn, +π the right. Left U-turn to right U-turn therefore sweeps
  back through straight instead of flipping sides; straight back keeps the
  side already bent toward, and spring overshoot stops at the U-turn.
- **Rendering:** plates are drawn once per resize; arrows redraw only while a
  finger is down or a spring is moving. Arrow paths are cached per half degree.
  Reduced motion snaps headings.
- **Model:** `direction/4/model/grid.ts`, with pure tests beside it. Not yet
  checked in a browser.
