# Traffic light 2

`/transportation/traffic-light/2`, 2026-10-06: an independent copy of
[`/1`](README.md) with the same pole repeated fifty times in a straight row
down one road (no road drawn). The question is what one ordinary object
becomes when repeated at street scale: a receding rhythm of arms and lamps.

Changed variable: pole count (1 → 50) along -z at a chosen spacing. Invariants:
every pole is `/1`'s model, options and timing plans; all poles switch
together on the same plan.

- `간격` 20 / 40 / 100 m (default 40 m): a dense downtown grid, an urban
  arterial, a suburban road. The row is 1–5 km long.
- The opening view stands on the kerb behind the first pole and looks down the
  row; fog runs from 60 m to 85 % of the row length, so the far poles dissolve
  into the horizon. Sky dome and ground reach 4 km; the dome is drawn first
  without depth so poles beyond it still show.
- The sun's shadow box (±30 m) sits just ahead of the camera and moves with
  every orbit; poles outside it cast no shadow. One box covering the whole row
  would leave shadows blurred beyond use.

## Performance

One pole is built in its own frame and repeated by instancing. Its steel and
clamps are merged per material and instanced once per pole; housings, boards
and lenses are instanced across all poles. Merged geometry is indexed
(`mergeVertices`), and curve segments on visors, door holes, gaskets and clamp
bands are lowered from `/1`'s.

Headless build, 50 poles:

| Configuration | Draw calls | Instances | Vertices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 head, 40 m | 11 | 550 | 0.91 M (3.63 M before indexing) |
| 3 four-colour heads + back + pole head, 20 m | 14 | 2,450 | 4.01 M (15.9 M before) |

Draw calls do not grow with pole or head count. The scene renders only on
signal change, orbit, option change or resize; orbiting also re-renders the
shadow pass. There is no per-instance distance culling or level of detail.

Unverified: not browser-checked; appearance at 1–5 km, fog balance and frame
time on devices are unknown. Lint, TypeScript and the navigation test pass.

Open question: a green wave (연동), offsetting each pole's plan by distance
over progression speed, would make the row's timing a relation rather than a
unison.
